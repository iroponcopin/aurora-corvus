2026-08-09

v1.8.6 — Added Missile Warhead & Phone App Icons

Visual updateBuilding & DecorGuns

Two fixes related to appearance and discoverability. Resolved the missile and nuclear warheads having zero textures by drawing dedicated icons from scratch and making them craftable. Also added the Missile Launcher, which players reported being unable to find, to the creative tab, and filled in the translations for its display name and control panel. The phone/computer's 7 apps also got dedicated new icons.

  • Drew dedicated 128px icons for the missile and nuclear warheads from scratch (previously there was no art at all — they showed up in your inventory as Minecraft's standard "purple-and-black checkerboard" missing-texture pattern).
  • Made the missile warhead (5 Iron Ingots + 1 TNT + 1 Redstone Block) and nuclear warhead (4 Blocks of Iron + 4 TNT + 1 Nether Star) craftable for the first time.
  • Added the Missile Launcher to the creative tab (crafting it was already possible before, but it never appeared in the tab, so it couldn't be found). Also added the display name and translations for its control panel screen — target coordinate entry, 9 status displays, 8 compass directions, the safety switch, and more (54 strings each in Japanese and English).
  • Drew dedicated new icons for all 7 phone/computer apps (camera feed, messages, lookup, weather, calculator, phone, camera) — previously it was just a row of colored squares. Also added per-app flourishes, like a precipitation icon and enlarged clock in the weather app, a recessed LCD-style design in the calculator app, a compass-needle display in the phone app, and a surveillance-monitor look for the camera feed app.
  • Improved it so opening an app now always shows that app's icon and name at the top of the screen.
⚠ Warnings
  • Making the missile and nuclear warheads craftable was a decision made by the dev team (not an explicit request from the owner). If the balance feels too strong or excessive, please talk to the admins.
  • No config deletion or editing needed this time either.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • The "Lookup" app's 3×3 recipe screen can't receive grid-placement data for ingredients from the server, so it falls back to a simplified display that packs ingredients in order from the top-left (the ingredient types and quantities themselves are correct).
  • The warheads' new icons, the control panel's translations, and the phone app's new screens are all still unconfirmed on the actual in-game screen.
  • 16 of the 17 Reinforced Glass Pane colors still don't have names set, a carryover issue from earlier versions.
2026-08-09

v1.8.5 — Fixed Water Pipe, Enhanced Beacon & Gas Mask Bugs

Bug fixBossBuilding & DecorSurvival

Fixed several bugs reported after v1.8.4: water pipes being unbreakable, the Enhanced Beacon Alpha/Beta not letting you pick abilities, and the gas mask appearing to float above the head. Also fixed the hostile mob spawn cap to correctly apply to the Overworld only, and changed the default to a flat 150 mobs.

  • Fixed water pipes being unbreakable. A unit mix-up meant the hitbox was only 1/32nd the visual thickness (hardness and tool requirements themselves were correct from the start).
  • Also fixed water pipe intakes standing alone in a river getting swept away and disappearing (redesigned as a waterlogging-capable block).
  • Fixed the Enhanced Beacon Alpha/Beta not letting you pick abilities. The screen would auto-close the very next tick after opening, and selections weren't being saved either (the standard Enhanced Beacon was unaffected).
  • Fixed the gas mask appearing to float above the head. Created a new dedicated 3D model and repositioned it to fit the face.
  • Reverted the Firework Doll's appearance from the 3D "plush toy" look added in v1.8.4 back to the flat art style it had through v1.8.3 (per the owner's request). No changes to functionality like the use-count limit.
  • Changed the Overworld hostile mob spawn cap from the previous "15x vanilla" (roughly 1,050 mobs) to a flat 150 mobs. Doesn't affect the Nether, the End, or the Sky (also fixed the earlier bug where this mistakenly applied to every dimension).

Balance changes

  • Hostile mob spawn cap (Overworld only): 15x vanilla (roughly 1,050 mobs) → a flat 150 mobs
⚠ Warnings
  • No config deletion or editing needed. The spawn cap value updates to the new default automatically.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • The new appearance of the water pipe and gas mask, and how the Enhanced Beacon GUI feels to use, haven't been confirmed in actual play yet.
  • Whether the new spawn density of 150 mobs feels right in actual play hasn't been verified yet.
2026-08-09

v1.8.4 — New Weapons, Phone OS, Water Pipes, Seasons & Sky Expansion

New featureBossBuilding & DecorGunsPowerSkySurvival

Added a big batch of features at once: 2 new weapons (Handgun and Submachine Gun), a Missile Launcher with 2 warhead types, real OS functionality for phones and computers, water pipes, an elevator that actually moves, and a spring/summer/fall/winter season system. Also raised the Overworld's build height ceiling (no change underground), and drastically reduced the list of sorting blocks and hoppers that required electricity.

  • Added 2 new weapons: the Handgun (200/sec, 12-round magazine, semi-auto) and Submachine Gun (450/sec, 40-round magazine, full-auto). Both use the existing small-caliber rounds, so no new ammo type was added.
  • Added the Missile Launcher (a placed weapon) along with Missile Warheads and Nuclear Warheads (the nuclear warhead has a cap on how many blocks it can destroy; both are Missile-Launcher-only and can't be fired while held).
  • Added a 2x zoom sight and lock-on feature to the Rocket Launcher (locks on after aiming for 1 second continuously; won't lock onto a target that's running).
  • Added real OS functionality to phones and computers (7 apps: camera feed, messages, lookup, weather, calculator, phone, and camera). Launch it by holding it and right-clicking, or pressing H — you can even take a photo and send it via message.
  • Added water pipes (supply water from a river or ocean to a cauldron or farmland). Also added an elevator whose car actually moves up and down.
  • Added Heavy-gauge Cable (reaches 4x the distance of regular cable), Shielded Cable (8x), and the LED Panel to the Power mod.
  • Completely reworked ore textures. Added the new material "Lavender Jade" (even rarer than Ruby), the Gas Mask, and the Aero Shield.
  • Added a season system (8 days per season, 32 days per year). Deserts in summer and snowy plains in winter are now harsher than before.
  • The Enhanced Beacon's Alpha and Beta tiers (Boss mod) are now craftable. Also, the base Enhanced Beacon no longer needs electricity (reverting the requirement added back in v1.8.1).
  • Raised the Overworld's build height ceiling from y=319 to y=1023 (no change to underground or the bottom of the world).
  • Drastically shrank the list of devices requiring electricity from 17 down to 3 (Void Trash block, laser emitter, auto turret). Hoppers, the 8 auto-sorting blocks, the conveyor belt, both traffic light types, and the automatic train stop block now all work without electricity.

Balance changes

  • Assault Rifle: measured 4,500 → 5,850 damage/sec
  • Minigun: measured 250 → 50 damage/sec (a heavy nerf — now weaker than a diamond sword)
  • Sniper Rifle and Heavy Sniper Rifle damage also increased. The durability calculation factor for sniper-class weapons also changed 0.5 → 0.6
⚠ Warnings
  • Rockets fired from the Missile Launcher keep destroying terrain even with mobGriefing set to false. To stop only the terrain destruction, set rocketDestroysTerrain to false in the config.
  • The Overworld's build height was extended to y=1023. This affects everyone, even if you never use the Sky dimension (no change underground).
  • Since the list of sorting blocks and hoppers requiring power was drastically shrunk, devices that had previously been stopped will automatically start working again. Watch out for unexpected reactivation in areas you haven't touched in a while.
  • The gun damage changes above are applied automatically. No config deletion or editing is needed, but you can revert them individually by specifying values in the config.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • Immediately after release, the Enhanced Beacon Alpha/Beta had a bug preventing ability selection (fixed in the next update).
  • Immediately after release, the Missile Launcher didn't show up in the creative tab and had no display name set. Icons for both warhead types were also missing (resolved in the next update).
  • The phone/computer apps didn't have dedicated icon art yet — just a row of colored squares (improved in the next update).

2026-08-05

v1.8.1 — Power Mod Added — Redstone Now Needs Electricity

New featureBossBuilding & DecorGunsPowerRailSkySurvivalVehicles

The 8th mod, "Power," joins the pack. In one of this project's biggest changes yet, vanilla redstone circuitry — redstone dust, repeaters, comparators, redstone blocks, and redstone torches — now needs electricity from a generator to function. Every existing redstone contraption, from auto-farms to stations to automatic doors, will shut down until you wire up a power grid.

  • The 8th mod, "Power," joins the pack. You can now build out a power grid with generators, nuclear plants, breaker panels, cables, and more.
  • 17 components — levers, buttons, pressure plates, tripwire hooks, observers, daylight sensors, sculk sensors, targets, trapped chests, detector rails, lecterns, jukeboxes, lightning rods, and more — still work without electricity, as before (manually opening/closing doors and gates works as usual too).
  • A redstone block will naturally break about 10 minutes after being placed, returning to your inventory as an item (this prevents a "free power source" that needs no electricity from just sitting around).
  • 3 special exceptions — the Void Trash block, laser emitter, and auto turret — are designed the opposite way: without power, they stay "active" as a safety measure (so you don't accidentally lose important items or mobs).
  • Fixed the Sniper Rifle, Heavy Sniper Rifle, and Laser Gun's projectile speed to the intended value (no change to damage, fire rate, magazine size, durability, or range).

Balance changes

  • Sniper Rifle / Heavy Sniper Rifle projectile speed: 16.0 → 24.0 (1.5x)
  • Laser Gun projectile speed: 16.0 → 48.0 (3x)
⚠ Warnings
  • Every existing setup that uses redstone circuitry — auto-farms, stations, automatic doors, and so on — will shut down. You'll need to wire up a power grid (generators and cables) from scratch.
  • To fully revert this change, set powerEnabled to false in config/sorakaze_power.json. To revert redstone specifically while keeping the rest, set redstoneRequiresPower to false in the same file.
  • Observers and daylight sensors are intentionally left needing no electricity, so you retain an in-game way to recover a base that's lost power.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • How it actually feels to play in a world where redstone stops working, and how thoroughly farms and stations end up shut down, are unverified.
  • The cables lighting up, the breaker panel's screen, and the appearance of the generator and nuclear plant haven't been confirmed on real hardware yet.
  • After switching power off and back on, an isolated cable not connected to a breaker panel can sometimes stay lit (breaking and replacing it fixes it).
2026-08-04

v1.8.0 — Config Auto-Repair (v1.8.0 Reissue)

Bug fixBossBuilding & DecorGunsSkySurvival

A fixed version reissued the same day. In the original v1.8.0, old values left over in earlier versions' config files were overwriting the new defaults, so things like the security camera's resolution, Excalibur's stats, and the Rocket Launcher's terrain destruction weren't taking effect. Going forward, you no longer need to manually delete config files with every update — a new mechanism automatically updates only "stale values set by an earlier version" to the current defaults on startup.

  • Introduced a mechanism that automatically detects "fossil" values left over from an earlier version and rewrites them to the current defaults. You no longer need to manually delete config files for future updates.
  • It never touches values you've tuned yourself — a value only gets updated if it matches a past version's default exactly.
  • With this fix, the security camera's resolution (768 → 3840), Excalibur's stats (attack power 12 → 75, attack speed 1.8 → 0.6), the Rocket Launcher's terrain destruction, and more now correctly take effect in actual play.
  • 5 mods were updated this time: Boss, Building Materials, Guns, Sky, and Survival. Rail and Vehicles are unchanged this time.
⚠ Warnings
  • This reissue ships under the same version number and filenames as the original v1.8.0. If you have the original v1.8.0, discard it and replace it with this version.
  • From now on, the "please delete your config files" step we used to mention with every update is no longer necessary.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • Excalibur's new stats and the Rocket Launcher's terrain destruction effect haven't been confirmed in actual play yet.

v1.7.7 — All 7 Mods Updated Together, Guns Return to Real Bullets

New featureBossBuilding & DecorGunsRailSkySurvivalVehicles

All 7 mods updated to v1.7.7 at the same time. Guns switched from hitscan (instant hit) back to actual traveling-projectile bullets (damage values themselves are unchanged). A new weapon, the "Rocket Launcher," a train automatic stop block, the Firework Doll, and many other new features were added across the mods.

  • [Guns] Bullets went back to actually traveling through the air. The visual feel changes, but each gun's damage value itself is unchanged.
  • [Guns] Added the new weapon "Rocket Launcher" and its dedicated ammo. It deals direct-hit plus explosion damage, and terrain destruction is off by default.
  • [Building Materials] Doors now close even with someone standing in the doorway. They're designed to have no hitbox while closing, so you won't take damage from being caught in one.
  • [Building Materials] Added colored Reinforced Glass Panes (17 colors), 5 decorative blocks (smartphone-, laptop-, and desktop-shaped), and a vault door. Also expanded the security camera's max video resolution (the default is unchanged).
  • [Boss] The number of special effects you can pick simultaneously on the Enhanced Beacon went from 3 to 5.
  • [Boss] "Excalibur," the drop from defeating Rock Lock, was rebuilt as a greatsword. It now has a dedicated 3D model, and its stats were buffed too.
  • [Sky] Fixed a bug where a ridden Dragon's body faced the opposite direction from its actual travel direction.
  • [Sky] Buffed Angel's Staff. The Royal Sword and the full set of Angel Gear also got reworked into more three-dimensional models (no stat changes).
  • [Rail] Added the new Automatic Train Stop block. Place it under a rail, and it will smoothly and automatically stop any approaching train.
  • [Vehicles] Fixed a bug where part of the vehicle body would stay invisible after using the first-person cockpit view once. Also reworked the appearance of 11 vehicle types.
  • [Survival] Roughly doubled the generation rate of Pearl Ore and Ruby Ore (newly generated land only). Added Block of Pearl and Block of Ruby.
  • [Survival] Added the new item "Firework Doll." Like a Totem of Undying, it's a charm that prevents death once, but it's more powerful and can be used up to 3 times. It can also be placed as decoration.

Balance changes

  • Excalibur (boss drop): damage 12.0 → 36.0 (3x), attack speed halved, reach 3.0 → 4.0 blocks, and it now applies knockback. However, its drop rate changed 100% → 70%.
  • Angel's Staff: damage 12 → 20, range 48m → 64m, shortened cooldown, melee bonus 7.0 → 10.0 (this is the first time it's surpassed Angel's Sword's melee bonus).
  • Rocket Launcher: 40.0 direct-hit damage plus explosion damage (explosion power equivalent to a creeper). Magazine size 1, and it has the slowest projectile speed of any gun.
⚠ Warnings
  • Config deletion needed: `sorakaze_boss.json` (Excalibur's stats), `sorakaze_sky.json` (Angel's Staff's stats). Without deleting them, the new numbers won't take effect.
  • We also recommend deleting `sorakaze_guns.json`. Without deleting it, there's a bug where the newly added "projectile speed" setting stays stuck at an abnormally slow value for every gun (this doesn't affect damage or other power-related numbers).
  • Of the 5 new decorative blocks, the smartphone-, laptop-, and desktop-shaped ones are modeled after real-world products, but both the in-game display and the install guide describe them only by their shape — no brand names are used.
  • [Balance] Even at this point, the Assault Rifle is still extremely strong at roughly 3,750 damage/sec — even with the Enhanced Beacon, Excalibur, and Angel's Staff all buffed, most bosses can still be killed in a few seconds. No resistance has been added to either guns or bosses.
  • Note: version numbers 1.4.3, 1.6.x, 1.7.2, and 1.7.6 were internal numbers used during development and were never actually distributed.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • How it feels for doors to now close even with someone standing there, how the Rocket Launcher's firing and impact feels, and how usable the Enhanced Beacon's new GUI is, are all unconfirmed on real hardware.
  • How Excalibur and Angel's Staff's new 3D models actually look, and how the Dragon's direction fix actually looks, are also unconfirmed.
  • The appearance of the Firework Doll, Pearl/Ruby blocks, and the Automatic Train Stop block, plus the PC performance impact of the security camera's higher resolution setting, are also unverified.
  • The Kraken and Titan's attack animations are still not implemented as originally specced (pending confirmation from the owner).
  • The 2 Sky mod issues found in v1.7.3 (raid lag, tamed dragons disappearing) are a separate matter from this update's Sky mod changes, and remain unresolved.

Bug fixBuilding & Decor

This is a Building-Materials-mod-only update. Fixed a bug where Reinforced Glass (all 17 colors) took a flat 75 seconds to mine regardless of tool, and a bug where the 50 large center-opening double doors added in v1.5.0 didn't visually swing all the way open.

⚠ Warnings
  • [Design limitation] The original goal was "only a dedicated axe can mine this faster," but due to how vanilla's systems work, it ended up so that any hoe — including a wooden one — mines it faster (scaled to its tier). We couldn't restrict it to one specific tool.

v1.7.1 — Emergency Fix for a Crash-on-Launch After Installing v1.7.0

Bug fixVehicles

Installing v1.7.0 caused the game to crash every single time it launched. The cause was in how the Vehicles mod registered its keybinds (landing gear, flaps, doors, and so on), and updating just the Vehicles mod to v1.7.1 resolves it.

  • Only the Vehicles mod (sorakaze_vehicles) was updated. The other 6 mods stay at v1.7.0 and are fine as-is.
  • No new items or blocks this time — this is purely a launch-crash fix.
⚠ Warnings
  • If you installed v1.7.0 and the game "crashes every time on launch," be sure to replace the Vehicles mod's jar file with this v1.7.1 version.
