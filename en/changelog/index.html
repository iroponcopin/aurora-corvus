V2.4.0 — Glass lanterns properly fixed, sanity no longer shares the screen with thirst, turret weapons are visible, and Glimpse Launcher arrives

New featureBackroomsBossBuilding & DecorGunsPlanarcadiaPowerRailSkySurvivalVehicles

The V2.2.1 glass lantern fix turned out not to be enough — a player sent a real screenshot showing the lanterns still reading as washed-out and near-white. We've redesigned them properly this time, removing the leftover white-hot centre entirely in favour of a single smooth colour gradient. In the Backrooms, the ordinary thirst bar was still showing up alongside sanity; it's now correctly hidden there. Weapons mounted on turrets were invisible even though they worked correctly; they now render. Several admin settings commands that were documented but didn't actually do anything have been wired up properly. And we're releasing a new desktop app, Glimpse Launcher, that checks for and applies pack updates automatically.

  • Redesigned the glass lantern glow again. V2.2.1's fix kept a small white-hot "filament" at the centre of each lantern, but in real play that filament still read as washed-out white regardless of colour — confirmed with a real in-game screenshot. The filament concept has been removed entirely in favour of a single smooth gradient from the centre to the edge. Still genuinely translucent glass.
  • Fixed the ordinary thirst bar showing up alongside sanity while inside the Backrooms. It's now correctly hidden there (the feature itself is unaffected everywhere else).
  • Fixed weapons mounted on turrets being invisible. Turrets have always accepted, fired, and returned mounted weapons correctly — they just never rendered. They now do.
  • Fixed several server admin settings commands that were documented but had no effect (village raid chance and wave count, video resolution cap, and others). The Backrooms module also gained its own admin settings entry point, which it previously had none of.
  • Released Glimpse Launcher, a new desktop app that checks the Wiki for pack updates, verifies downloads by SHA-256, takes an automatic backup before applying an update, and can open the official Minecraft Launcher for you (you still choose the Fabric profile yourself, exactly as before). One file runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux (Java is required to run it). Get it from the Download page.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • This is Glimpse Launcher's first release. The launcher cannot yet update itself automatically — that's planned for a future version.

New featureBackroomsBossBuilding & DecorGunsPlanarcadiaPowerRailSkySurvivalVehicles

This round adds an 11th mod, Sapporo — a dimension recreating the real city of Sapporo, Japan, from real survey data. Backrooms Level 1, "The Lobby", is now a real explorable space rather than a demo room, with a glass objective panel shown on the left of the screen at all times. Village raids can now trigger on pure chance without Bad Omen, turrets let you mount guns and bows, video now plays up to 4K and stops automatically when you walk away, the Sparxie staff gained a ranged attack alongside its melee one, the minigun and rocket launcher were rebalanced, a flyable camera drone and its own phone app arrived, and the Building & Decor mod gained 52 new glass lanterns.   • The Sapporo mod (sorakaze_sapporo) has finally shipped. It's a dimension recreating the real city of Sapporo, Japan, from real geographic data.   • Backrooms Level 1, "The Lobby", is now a real, walkable, explorable space rather than a small demo room. You reach it through a rift that generates naturally near an End City.

  • The Amethyst Gate can now be built from plain vanilla amethyst blocks as well as Portal Amethyst Blocks — the two can even be mixed in the same frame.

  • Video playback now supports up to 4K, always keeps a 16:9 aspect ratio, and stops automatically once you walk far enough away from the screen.
  • The Sparxie staff can now attack at range (a 24-block bolt) as well as in melee.
  • The minigun now hits roughly three times as hard, and the rocket launcher's blast is far more powerful than before.

  • The Building & Decor mod gained 52 new glass lanterns (18 vanilla-glass, 17 reinforced, 17 blackout). Full light level 15, and measured brighter than the existing LED panel.
  • The admin app's PC screen gained a change-log view for the server administrator — a read-only view of past changes. Viewing it is not itself logged.

Balance changes

  • Minigun damage roughly tripled.
  • Rocket launcher blast power greatly increased.
⚠ Warnings
  • Playing in the Sapporo dimension requires the server administrator to have installed a separate map data file on the server (not distributed on this site). Without it, that world simply isn't reachable.
  • Backrooms entities cannot be defeated. Don't try to fight them with ordinary weapons — evade instead.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • Most Sapporo building heights are still a district-level statistical estimate rather than real survey data. They'll be updated once more detailed 3D city data becomes available.
  • Whether players can build and light their own Heaven gate (glowstone portal) depends on a server-administrator setting, which defaults to off.

V2.1.0 — Backrooms Levels 3–8 become real, endless levels; a server-admin app on the in-game PC; boss summon recipes reach the recipe book

New featureBackroomsBossBuilding & DecorGunsPlanarcadiaPowerRailSkySurvivalVehicles

The largest change this round is Backrooms Levels 3 through 8. They used to be 128-block demo islands; they are now endless levels you can actually walk into and explore. The in-game PC in the Building & Decor mod gained a server-administration app, and in the Boss mod the summoning items' recipes now appear in the in-game recipe book. The three data-driven guns each have their own in-hand model at last.

  • Only four mods actually changed: Backrooms, Building & Decor, Boss and Guns. The other six (Planarcadia, Power, Rail, Sky, Survival, Vehicles) were bumped to 2.1.0 only to keep the set consistent — their jars differ from the 2.0.0 ones in exactly one entry, the version string in fabric.mod.json. Not a byte of their content changed.
  • Backrooms Levels 3 to 8 went from 128-block demo islands to real, endless levels. None of them has an edge any more; all of them can be walked and explored.
  • Level 3, the Electrical Station. The current is live and touching a panel is dangerous. A Hound patrols, and a Smiler waits in the underground cable room.
  • Level 4, the Empty Offices. Rated safe — but 3% of the windows are watching you, and they look exactly like the rest. Sanity slowly recovers here. The almond water is in the break room.
  • Level 5, the Terror Hotel. 80% of the numbered doors are locked. In each contiguous region exactly one room differs in its details. Do not linger.
  • Level 6, Lights Out. A flashlight works properly for 20 seconds; past that it only works about 15% of any given second. While it is lit, entities notice you from four times as far — light attracts. This is a level you navigate by ear.
  • Level 7, Thalassophobia. One house of fixed shape, and an ocean with no far shore. Do not walk to the end of the pier.
  • Level 8, the Cave System. Tunnels dug for people and then abandoned; the iron rails really do lead somewhere. There is a flooded winze 32 blocks deep.
  • The in-game PC gained a "Server Admin" app. Without opening a config file, an administrator can change: whether phone messaging, calls and the browser are allowed; how many camera feeds can be shown at once and whether live feeds are allowed; whether automatic backups run, how often, and how many are kept; and a set of boss knobs (Plague Doctor natural spawn and its chance, thunderstorm-only spawning, respawn cooldown, Plague Wither pack size, wardens required for the Sculk Golem, the Reaper's wither summon count, whether Rock Lock spawners generate in nether fortresses, Plague Doctor max health, Plague Wither health multiplier).
  • Opening the admin app needs an admin ID and a password. The server administrator hands those out individually, only to the people they choose. They are not written anywhere on this site. Sign-in expires after a while, and failed attempts are rate-limited.
  • The recipes for the Boss mod's summoning items (Forge Altar Core, Molten Crown, Sculk Heart, Sonar Eye, Frost Altar, Primal Egg, Soul Blood Altar) now appear in the in-game recipe book. Until now they had no unlock path at all, so there was no way to discover how to make them from inside the game.
  • Four discovery advancements were added as a hint toward summoning: The Scorching Key, The Fathom Key, The Frost Key and The Altar Is Built. Each fires when you hold the matching summoning item.
  • The Plague Doctor's natural spawn is available again, behind an administrator toggle — but it defaults to OFF. Until an administrator explicitly turns it on, behaviour is identical to V2.0.0.
  • The three data-driven guns (Service Pistol, Marksman Carbine, Breaching Shotgun) each have their own in-hand model now. All three had been borrowing the assault rifle's mesh, so holding a pistol showed you a rifle.
⚠ Warnings
  • Levels 3 to 8 generate in a fundamentally different way now. In a world where you visited the demo islands under V2.0.0, the terrain in that area will not match what was there before.
  • The admin ID and password for the Server Admin app are never published on this site. Get them from the server administrator directly.

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point

  • The Sapporo mod (sorakaze_sapporo) is not part of this round. It is deferred to V2.2.
  • The actual look, sound and entity behaviour of Levels 3–8 had not been confirmed in a real play-through at the time of writing.

New featureBackroomsBossBuilding & DecorGunsPlanarcadiaPowerRailSkySurvivalVehicles

This is V2 proper. The 10th mod, Planarcadia, shipped here for the first time. The Boss mod gained four new bosses, each with its own summoning ritual; Survival gained water infrastructure and a refrigerator; Vehicles gained gasoline; Power gained an air purifier. The Backrooms grew a great deal, and Building & Decor gained blackout reinforced glass in every colour.

  • A 10th mod, Planarcadia (sorakaze_planarcadia), is bundled for the first time. Build a frame out of Portal Amethyst Blocks in the same shape as a Nether portal, light it, and it opens onto a pleasure world where night rules. It brings its own blocks — LED signs, the phantom moon, the Interplanar Jump Mural, a shop counter. It does not depend on any other Glimpse Alpha mod.
  • Four new bosses joined the Boss mod: the Volcanic Golem King (Forge Altar Core + Molten Crown), the Abyssal Kraken Warden (Sculk Heart + Sonar Eye), the Prismatic Ice Phoenix Dragon (Frost Altar + Primal Egg), and the Witherstorm Demon Overlord (Soul Blood Altar + 3 wither skeleton skulls + a nether star).
  • The Soul Blood Altar refuses the Witherstorm Demon Overlord until all three of the others have been killed. Each of the four has a guaranteed drop: the Volcanic Core, the Abyssal Sinew, the Prismatic Plume and the Overlord's Heart.

Put a gun and its attachments into the Gun Workbench and they are fitted the moment you take the gun back out; put a fitted gun back in and they come off. v1.9.0 — New Mod "Backrooms" Joins the Pack, Wither Storm Overhauled

New featureBackroomsBossSurvival

The 9th mod, "Backrooms," has joined the pack — a sanity-driven exploration experience reached through rifts that spawn near End Cities. The boss mod's Wither Storm has been completely overhauled with a new look, growth stages, and a new status effect, plus new guaranteed drops. The other 6 mods (building & decor, guns, power, sky, rail, vehicles) are unchanged.

  • The 9th mod, "Backrooms" (sorakaze_backrooms), has joined the pack. Every time an End City generates, exactly one rift (an altar) spawns nearby in a safe spot — one per City (existing End Cities don't get one).
  • Features a sanity system. It maxes out at 100 and drains to zero in about 18 minutes if you do nothing. Drinking the restorative item found in chests recovers 35. Roughly every 30 seconds there's a 12% chance of a "blackout," and things start to change once sanity drops below 20.
  • You can't place blocks inside the maze, and the walls, floor, and ceiling — including the chests — can't be destroyed at all. Other dimensions are unaffected.
  • The entity "Hollow Listener" roams the maze (40 HP, 6 attack damage. It freezes while you're looking at it and moves again once you look away). Up to 3 can be present at once.
  • A 3-stage progress tracker (① confirm you're alive → ② gather traces → ③ escape) stays on screen at all times.
  • Completely remade the Wither Storm boss's appearance. Replaced the old fixed-HP, single-stage design with a growth-stage system (S0 Embryo → S1 Gorging → S2 Hunting → S3 Rampage → S4 Tempest → S5 Zenith, HP ranging 600–24,000).
