V4.2

Ship freeze and QA gate

Ship freeze and QA gate

No new place, no new boss. This release makes V4.2.0 through V4.2.5 shippable: the Ashen Conurbation's blocks and items get their tags, a tester's tool and three commands arrive, and two holes nobody had ever seen are closed — six spawn eggs and a bucket that appeared in no creative tab, and the asset and translation checks that were never wired into the build.

Highlights (8)

  • Eleven tag tables now cover the Ashen Conurbation's blocks and items (city fabric, route decks, the pressure network, contaminants, the filter kit, salvage, city hostiles, and the tools that break the ring wall). Each one has a test that resolves it against the live registry and counts what it finds
  • New Inspector Ledger: right-click it to read out where you are — biome, height, whether you are on a route deck, whether the ring is charged, whether the colossus is slain, and the content-freeze flag. Sneak-use re-measures nine places in the city. No recipe; creative tab only
  • Three new commands: /fallout help (the registered verbs), /fallout assert (checks that nine parts of the city generated correctly) and /fallout freeze (records the content freeze). All at permission level 2
  • A new hidden advancement, Frozen Line, for holding the Inspector Ledger
  • Six spawn eggs and the molten residue bucket now appear in the creative tab (eggs grouped last). Those seven items had appeared in no tab at all, and the test meant to catch it built its expectation from the same list the tab used, so it could only ever pass
  • When the dimension is not loaded, /fallout viaduct, drop and cradle and the ashen gateway now print a real sentence instead of a raw translation key — a defect that had shipped since V4.2.0
  • The header tank, the salvage bay and the kinetic seizure machine now need an iron pickaxe or better to drop. A wooden pickaxe used to collect them
  • Walking on condensate film now sounds like walking through shallow water

Balance changes (2)

  • No new block, mob, area or fluid. What arrived is one tester item, one advancement and the tag tables
  • Only the Ashen Conurbation mod changed. The other twelve are untouched inside; only their version numbers move to 4.2.6

Warnings (3)

  • The header tank, salvage bay and kinetic seizure machine now require an iron pickaxe or better. If you meant to collect one with a wooden pickaxe, do it before updating
  • The Filter Ring wall keeps its hardness of 50 and still needs a diamond pickaxe. The brief asked for hardness 8 and an iron pickaxe; that would make a shipped block easier to mine in live worlds, which is not something to do in a release whose purpose is a freeze. Say the word and it changes in the next one
  • The content-freeze flag lives on the overworld's saved data, so it survives deleting the Ashen Conurbation dimension

The Cradle Economy and the Colossus's three phases

The Vein Pit becomes a fight with a bill. A marked Pit Cut runs from the Filter Ring's south gate down into the bowl, and the floor of the pit is now a Cradle plate with a salvage bay on it. The Assembly Colossus fights in three named, coloured phases, and its seizure core drops only if you land the killing blow standing on the plate. At the bay, eight chisel strokes break the core into parts the city's routes can use. This release also fixes a shipped defect: phase-three molten residue never despawned and permanently flooded the pit floor.

Highlights (11)

  • New route, the Pit Cut: a five-wide way from the Filter Ring's south gate at (0,64,128) down to the floor of the Vein Pit — a cutting at grade as far as the rim, then a stair that follows the bowl down one step at a time, with retaining walls and a lamp every twelve blocks on the west shoulder
  • Three caches along the cut (z=96, 72, 52). The one nearest the gate always holds a cradle chisel, so the salvage bay is never gated behind crafting after a first death
  • New area, the Cradle Floor: a plate disk of radius 14 on the pit floor, three cover pillars at 120°, and the salvage bay on its north edge. The ground under the plate is filled, so breaking one tile does not drop you; the plate is slightly slipperier than stone
  • The Assembly Colossus now wakes rather than idling: it stays still until a player comes within 28 blocks and is either on the plate or inside radius 16 below y=28. Then the phase sound plays and the fight begins
  • Three phases: SEIZE (above 60% health, a three-wide smash every 60 ticks), ASSEMBLY (30–60%, smash every 80 ticks, two scrap sentinels at a time up to three alive, and a ranged spit every 70 ticks for 8 damage), and BLEED (below 30%, no new sentinels, six molten residue blocks on a radius-6 ring every 100 ticks, faster walk, a four-wide smash every 50 ticks)
  • The boss bar changes name and colour with the phase — purple for SEIZE, yellow for ASSEMBLY, red for BLEED
  • Standing on the Pit Cut at y≥40 halves the smash damage you take. It is a falloff, not immunity — and sentinels will not chase you up the cut above y=40
  • The Salvage Bay: use a seizure core on it and it opens for 200 ticks; land eight cradle-chisel hits inside that window and it yields 6 stabilised residue, 1 kinetic seizure, 2 condensate vials, 1 viaduct token and 1 ring main schematic. Miss the window and the bay spits the core back out
  • Three new items: the Seizure Core (stack of one, the colossus's unique drop), the Cradle Chisel (durability 64; iron speed on the plate, the bay and residue veins, wood speed everywhere else) and the Rim Beacon (three uses; a 20-second Filter Grace pulse, only at y≥56 inside the Filter Ring or on the Pit Cut)
  • Three new advancements — Cut the Pit, Strip the Motor, and Three Motions (slay the colossus after seeing every phase)
  • The survey map gains south tick marks, and /fallout cradle drops you onto the plate (not on top of the colossus)

Balance changes (2)

  • The colossus keeps 320 health, 14 melee and 8 ranged. What changed is the phase structure, and the sentinel cap moving from four alive to three
  • Only the Ashen Conurbation mod changed. The other twelve are untouched inside; only their version numbers move to 4.2.5

Warnings (7)

  • ⚠ The seizure core drops only if the killing blow comes from a player standing on the cradle plate. Kill it from the rim or from range and the core sinks with the body — the slain flag still sets, so there is no second fight and that world's core is gone for good. Come down and fight it
  • Fixed: phase-three molten residue never despawned and permanently flooded the cradle floor on the first fight. Placed residue now clears after ten seconds, and the ring never lands on the salvage bay or the plate's centre 3×3
  • The Pit Cut and the Cradle Floor appear in existing Ashen Conurbation worlds only in chunks that have not been generated yet; an already-generated pit will show neither
  • The cut crosses the ring main's +Z standpipe at z=120, which stands in the middle of the way. Walk around it — breaking it costs the ring its pressure
  • The cut's lamps are generated unlit. They sit outside the pressure network's eligibility radius, so charging the ring will not light them: the pit stays dark
  • The Rim Beacon's recipe consumes a filter lamp, and filter lamps have no recipe and appear in no chest — the only source is mining one out of the Filter Ring, whose lit lamps have been the ring's safety since V4.2.4. Finished beacons also drop from the Pit Cut caches at 8%
  • A filled salvage bay cannot be mined; otherwise the bay would break before the eighth chisel stroke landed

The Ring Main and lamp pressure, every door armoured, and four fixes

The Filter Ring's lamps stop being magic. They burn condensate pushed from a header tank in the Sump Hall around a buried main at radius 120, and when the tank runs dry the ring goes dark. A new Ring Gallery lets you walk the inner wall at y=70. Alongside it, four reported problems are fixed: nobody could tell who sells Sparxie's staff, the Echolic Locator crashed the game on every use, the twelve doors this pack adds were not as strong as the vault door, and chemically strengthened glass was broken in one bite by the Wither Storm despite promising eight minutes.

Highlights (13)

  • The Filter Ring's lamps now burn condensate. Fill the header tank in the Sump Hall (sixteen buckets), keep the north-wall seizure machines running and the pressure valve open, and the ring lights; let the tank run dry and the ring goes dark
  • New area, the Ring Gallery: a two-wide walkway at y=70 on the inner face of the wall, all the way round, with a ladder at each of the four gates. Like the viaduct deck, no new residue sickness starts while you are on it
  • New route, the Ring Main: a closed loop buried at radius 120, y=62, with a standpipe in each of the four gate roads. Two new blocks — the Header Tank (two blocks tall; fill it with a condensate bucket, draw it off with an empty one) and the Pressure Valve (sneak to open or close; redstone forces it open)
  • New item, the Ring Main Schematic (paper over a sump gauge): while you hold it the ring's state is on your action bar and a dashed circle at radius 120 appears on the survey map. Two new advancements — Light the Ring, and Walk the Gallery (visit all four gate ledges)
  • Sparxie now stands behind the counter in Planarcadia. The name tag is always visible and the gold outline shows through the glass, so you can see the shop from across the plaza. Right-clicking Sparxie is exactly the counter's purchase: the first click quotes, a second within thirty seconds buys the Downvote Stick
  • The counter is now Sparxie's Counter, and holding it shows three lines: who sells here, the list price of 200,000 emeralds times this world's multiplier, and that payment comes from your inventory plus containers within six blocks. The stick's own tooltip now names the shop too
  • Fixed: holding the Echolic Locator crashed the game. From V4.0.0 to V4.2.3 the client died on the very next frame after any creature made a sound, so nobody has ever seen what this tool draws
  • What it draws: a cyan 1×1×1 box around the position of the sound, fading out over about three seconds. Only creature sounds within 32 blocks (hostile, neutral, players) — never rain, music, records or block sounds. Put the locator away and any boxes vanish at once
  • All twelve doors this pack adds now follow the vault door's rule: the ten kit doors get destroy speed -1 and blast resistance 3,600,000. No boss, raid, gunfire, TNT, warhead, ender dragon, wither, earthquake, drill or piston can break them
  • Only a player gets through, with a pickaxe, axe, shovel or hoe, in exactly three minutes — tier, efficiency and haste make no difference, and bare hands or a sword make no progress at all. The door drops for any of those four tools now (before, anything but a pickaxe dug through and dropped nothing)
  • Chemically strengthened glass keeps its eight-minute promise. Neither the Wither Storm, nor a warhead, nor a sky raid, nor an earthquake takes it in one hit; a raid's diggers now need eight minutes rather than a hundred seconds
  • The Wither Storm no longer grows on blocks it did not actually break, and no longer records them in the restore journal. The Wither Storm and the Witherstorm Demon Overlord are now registered siege bosses
  • New administrator commands: /fallout pressure (the network's nodes, charge, tank contents and lit lamps for the block you are looking at) and /chemglass admin remove <pos> / info <pos> (permission level 3; every use is logged and announced to the operators present)

Balance changes (2)

  • No Ashen Conurbation number changed (the sickness clock, the mask's durability, the colossus's health). What changed is the condition for the Filter Ring being safe: its lamps have to be lit
  • Only five mods changed (building, Ashen Conurbation, survival, Planarcadia, boss). The other eight are untouched inside; only their version numbers move to 4.2.4

Warnings (11)

  • ⚠ A newly generated city is born with a dark ring, on purpose. While the lamps are out the Filter Ring plaza is contaminated, so bring a mask on your first visit — fill a bucket from the Sump Hall pool and pour it into the header tank to light it
  • A lit ring burns 40 mB per second. The pool's 25 buckets and whatever the chests roll will not last for ever, and nothing refills them automatically in this version. The 46 lamps on the Ashen Viaduct and in the Condenser Reach are not on the ring main and stay lit either way
  • The Ring Main and the Ring Gallery appear in existing Ashen Conurbation worlds only in chunks that have not been generated yet; an already-generated centre will show neither
  • Kit doors used to be breakable by bosses and raiders. From V4.2.4 they are not — a base designed around them being broken through needs rethinking. The rule applies to doors already placed, and taking one down now costs three minutes
  • This covers only the twelve doors the pack adds. Vanilla's 21 doors and the backrooms' 5 are unchanged
  • Removing siege glass is refused while it still has durability, on every path that deletes a block: the storm's terrain feeding, boss impacts, Malachor's magma, warheads, raid diggers, earthquakes, and /setblock and /fill as well. Only player mining is unchanged — hardness decides there
  • "Sustained assault" is counted on the wall clock. A refused removal takes off only the time elapsed since the assault last landed, up to two minutes. So a single warhead costs the glass nothing, and only sustained fire wears it down; when the attack stops the clock stops, and accumulated damage never heals
  • Chemically strengthened glass cubes can no longer be pushed by pistons (like obsidian) — pushing them back and forth was healing the siege damage. The universal harvester can no longer collect them either; the original rule (a diamond-or-better pickaxe with silk touch) applies again
  • The V4.1.1 note saying the siege-boss tag ships empty because this pack has no Wither Storm was wrong, and because of it the glass's eight minutes had never once been in force
  • The V4.0.0 description of the Echolic Locator was also wrong: it draws no direction line. It outlines the position of a sound heard within 32 blocks, and the box lasts about three seconds
  • The nameless citizen that used to hover inside the shop hut is gone. Looking like a shopkeeper while doing nothing when clicked was the root of "who actually sells this?". Note that the price you pay is not the list price but the list price times this world's multiplier (×10 to ×100)

The Filter Underworks and the Service Drop, plus the Padlock and the PIN Door Lock

The Ashen Conurbation gains a sixth precinct directly under the Filter Ring, the Filter Underworks, and the Service Drop that leads down to it from the plaza grate: the shaft, the east service tunnel, the Sump Hall with its condensate pool, the pipe leeches that drop from the pressure mains, the service cage, the service key, the sump gauge and the condensate bucket. Alongside it the building mod gains a Padlock and a PIN Door Lock. They hang on every door in the pack, the 21 vanilla doors included, and a locked door cannot be broken by players, mobs, bosses, TNT, pistons or commands. A padlock opens, closes and breaks only for the person who hung it and for operators; a PIN door lock only for those who have entered its 4–8 digit number.

Highlights (13)

  • The Filter Underworks (sixth precinct): an underground layer directly beneath the Filter Ring, y40–63 within 96 blocks of the centre. Contamination continues there — no filter grace. Below y64 the survey map shows an inner circle and a centre mark
  • The Service Drop: from the sump grate in the middle of the plaza at (0,64,0) a shaft goes down to y49, with a ladder and a redstone-powered service cage (sneak-use inside it to ride between y49 and y65, ten ticks apart; unpowered it says the cage has no charge). For testers, /fallout drop lands you inside the cage
  • The east service tunnel: three blocks wide at y50–52 from x=4 to the first viaduct pier at x=132, with a pressure main on the ceiling every four blocks and a service locker at x=64 (one service key guaranteed plus 3–5 items). The ladder at its end climbs to the viaduct deck (y72)
  • The Sump Hall (a sump-brick box, x −10..10, y48–59, z −10..10): a 5×5 condensate pool, four condenser coils, two kinetic seizure machines and three chests (5–8 items). Standing there grants "Take the Drop"
  • Pressure mains and pipe leeches: one main in five is infested; walk under one within four blocks above eye level and a leech drops from the ceiling (health 10, attack 2, speed 0.24, climbs walls). A bite gives 4 s of residue sickness and 2 s of slowness. It drops 0–1 condensate vial and a service key one time in five. Underworks spawn table: pipe leech 80 (2–5), scrap sentinel 5 (1), nothing else
  • Condensate (a fluid) and the condensate bucket: contact gives 6 s each of Sump Damp (slowness I + mining fatigue I) and residue sickness. It cannot be placed in or flow into the Residue Flats or the Vein Pit — only the ring and the underworks — and molten residue can no longer be placed in the underworks either. Right-click a condensate source with a condensate vial to refill it to 8 uses (the source stays). Filling a bucket in the underworks grants "Prime the Sump"
  • Service Key (2 iron ingots + 1 iron nugget, no durability): the only thing that opens and closes the sump grate — hands and redstone do nothing to it. Sump Gauge (6 iron ingots + sump grate + condensate vial): held in the main hand it shows on the action bar how many pressure mains are within 16 blocks and how many of them are charged by a kinetic seizure machine. Sump Brick ×4 = 3 precast concrete + 1 condensate vial
  • Sump mist drifts through the underworks, and three sounds arrive with it (cage, grate, leech drop). The Assembly Colossus does not wake for players inside the Sump Hall, the shaft or the tunnel
  • Padlock (5 iron ingots): right-click a door you placed yourself. Only you and operators (permission level 2 or above) can open, close, dig or break it; for everyone else the door stays shut and no cracks appear. Sneak + empty hand as the owner takes it off and drops the padlock
  • PIN Door Lock (6 iron ingots + stone button + redstone dust): right-click opens a keypad; choose 4–8 digits and confirm with #. Only people who have entered the number can open, close or break the door. A wrong number starts a lockout on that lock (1 second, doubling up to 10 minutes, for everyone); the right number lights the green lamp for two seconds and opens the door
  • A locked door does not break: mining, TNT and warhead explosions, the wither, pistons, removing the floor under it, even /setblock over it — the door stays and nothing drops. Redstone does not open it and zombies stop pounding on it. When an allowed person breaks it, one door and one lock drop
  • Every door counts: the 12 wooden, the iron and the 8 copper vanilla doors, and the building mod's 2 armoured and 10 kit doors. The lock hangs on the face where you clicked and swings with the leaf; on the hatch it sits on the plate
  • Only the person who placed the door (and operators) can lock it, so nobody can seal someone else's house. Doors placed before this version, and the city's doors, can be locked by operators only. An open door cannot be locked

Balance changes (2)

  • No Ashen Conurbation number changed (the sickness clock, the mask's durability, the colossus's health). The underworks are contaminated exactly like the Collapsed Precincts, with no grace
  • Only the building mod and the Ashen Conurbation mod changed. The other eleven mods are untouched inside (only their version numbers move to 4.2.3)

Warnings (7)

  • The Ashen Conurbation mod's pickaxe tags (mineable/pickaxe and needs_diamond_tool) had been written in the wrong place since V4.2.0, so vanilla pickaxes never read them. V4.2.3 fixes this: blocks that require a tool now drop correctly with a pickaxe, and the Filter Ring's wall needs a diamond pickaxe (a behaviour change)
  • The underworks appear in existing Ashen Conurbation worlds only in chunks that have not been generated yet. A centre that is already generated will show neither the shaft nor the hall
  • The fog colour when submerged in condensate (#A0E6E2) is not implemented — 26.2 has no per-fluid fog hook — so the biome's fog applies
  • A door can be locked only by the person who placed it (or an operator). Doors placed before V4.2.3 carry no placer record: ask an operator if you want one locked
  • A locked copper door stops weathering (honeycomb and axes do nothing) until the lock comes off
  • For administrators: /doorlock admin remove <x y z> (permission level 3) removes a lock without breaking the door; every use is logged and announced to the operators present
  • The lock items are in the "building" creative tab. Trapdoors and fence gates cannot be locked

The Condenser Reach and the Ashen Viaduct, plus a clean sweep of the doors

The Ashen Conurbation gains the Condenser Reach to the east and the Ashen Viaduct that runs there from the Filter Ring's east gate: the Bleed Shed at its end, four waystations along the way, the Coil Crawler, the Viaduct Token and the Condensate Vial. Alongside it the building mod's doors are replaced wholesale: every door of V4.2.0 and earlier (102 of them) is removed and ten new doors built from the design renders take their place. The V4.2.1 Wheel Vault Door and Riveted Hatch can no longer be destroyed by any boss, mob, TNT or warhead; a player digs through in three minutes.

Highlights (8)

  • Ten new doors, each reproduced from one design render, all animated: the Studded Steel Double Gate (4×3), the Hangar Blast Gate (6×4, slides sideways into its pocket), the Keypad Security Door (2×3, the lever drops first), the X-Braced Vault Door (3×4, two turns of the wheel), the Iron-Bound Double Door (3×5, the bar slides free first), the Gear-Driven Gate (3×4, brass gears lift a stone slab), Blast Door 01 (5×4, lifts into the lintel), the Round Airlock Door (3×3, four clamps turn free first), the Portcullis (3×4, winches turn) and the Cell Door (2×3, the latch slides first). Right-click or redstone for all of them
  • The Wheel Vault Door and the Riveted Hatch are indestructible: no boss, mob, wither, dragon, raid, heavy sniper, TNT or nuclear warhead can break them. Only a player can, with a pickaxe, axe, shovel or hoe, in exactly three minutes whatever the tool's tier (bare hands and swords make no progress)
  • The Ashen Viaduct: from the ring's east gate (x=128) to the Condenser Reach (x=480), a five-wide deck runs unbroken at y=72 with a rail on the centre line, filter lamps every 16 blocks along both edges and a pier every 12 blocks, cutting a corridor through towers and rubble. While you stand on the deck no new Residue Sickness sets in (an existing one keeps ticking down). Wind every 80 ticks
  • Four waystations (north side at x=192, 256, 320, 384): bleached-precast booths with a collapsed window, a chest (iron nuggets, condensate vials, a viaduct token, crystals, coal, bread) and a condenser coil on the roof. Two in five carry a Coil Crawler marker that spawns one crawler, once, when you come within 32 blocks
  • The Bleed Shed (x=496 to 516, 21×13×21): its west face opens onto the viaduct; inside, six condenser coils, two chests (vials, tokens, stabilised residue, a filter mask, a survey map), a Kinetic Seizure in an iron-bar bay and a 3×3 cistern under the floor. Forty ticks inside grant Wet Coil for 8 seconds (Haste I, and Residue Sickness drains twice as fast); the ceiling drips
  • The Coil Crawler (14 health, 3 damage, 0.28 speed): neutral to anyone on the deck, hostile to anyone who steps down onto the Reach, a pier or a coil. It climbs walls and coils and drops 0-2 condensate vials and 0-1 residue crystal. Reach spawns: crawlers 70 (packs of 2-4), stalkers 15, no pulse hounds
  • The Viaduct Token (right-click on the deck for 30 seconds of Speed I with a 30-second cooldown; elsewhere it says the token only wakes on the viaduct) and the Condensate Vial (8 uses: residue and residue veins become bleached precast, buds vanish, a molten residue source vanishes, contaminated asphalt becomes viaduct deck; the ring wall and the seizure are untouched). Recipes: token = 4 iron nuggets + 4 vials + stabilised residue; vial ×2 = glass bottle + water bucket + condenser coil; bleached precast ×4 = 2 precast + 2 vials; viaduct deck ×4 = 4 bleached; stonecutter: bleached → pier
  • The Survey Map's scale widens to 512 so the Reach, the viaduct line and the terminus fit on the sheet (the diamond is unchanged). Two advancements (Follow the Heat Line, Still Condensing). /fallout viaduct takes testers to the east gate deck

Balance changes (2)

  • The V4.2.1 numbers, the colossus's health and the mask's durability are unchanged. On the Reach the sickness clock is the Collapsed Precincts' (10 s bare, 40 s masked), but it stops on the deck
  • Only the building mod and the city mod changed; nothing inside the other eleven mods moved (the version number alone goes to 4.2.2)

Warnings (5)

  • ⚠ The 102 doors of V4.2.0 and earlier (the automatic doors, fusuma, large doors, hatch, camouflage door, roller shutter, sliding glass door, jail bars and the 93 generated doors) are removed. Old doors placed in an existing world disappear (they become air) when it loads with this build, and old door items vanish from inventories. This is a wholesale replacement at the owner's instruction
  • The automatic door's power settings (autoDoorRequiresPower and three more) are gone from the config. The other powered devices (security camera, elevator, escalator, ATM) are unchanged
  • Condenser coils drop only with a pickaxe or silk touch; other tools drop nothing
  • In an existing city world the viaduct appears in chunks that have not been generated yet, not in chunks already generated
  • Everything from V4.2.3 onward (the Underworks and so on) is not in this build

Finishing the Ashen Conurbation, plus the Wheel Vault Door and Riveted Hatch

A small update that gives the systems V4.2 laid down their look and feel. The city's sky becomes a perpetual dusk with no stars and no moon, and the fog thickens district by district. Residue buds grow, the Kinetic Seizure's shaft turns, and the Survey Map draws the city on your screen. Three bugs are fixed and three numbers tuned. Alongside it, the building mod gains the Wheel Vault Door (3×3) and the Riveted Hatch (2×2) built from the design renders, both animated: the vault door turns its hand-wheel and draws its bolts before the leaf swings.

Highlights (7)

  • The Wheel Vault Door (3×3) and the Riveted Hatch (2×2, floor or ceiling): armoured doors reproduced piece by piece from the 3D design renders. Right-click the vault door and its hand-wheel turns one and a half times, six bolts withdraw, and only then does the leaf swing open 90 degrees (three seconds). The hatch lifts its handle and then opens 100 degrees. Both work with redstone, and their collision disappears while open, so nothing gets crushed. Recipes: 4 stone bricks + 4 iron blocks around 1 netherite scrap for the vault door; 4 iron nuggets + 4 iron ingots around 1 iron block for the hatch
  • The Ashen Conurbation has a sky of its own. The sun hangs just above the western horizon and never moves; there is no moon and there are no stars. The zenith is purple-black, the horizon brown, the clouds dark, and the sun a weak brown-purple disc. Fog differs by precinct: the Filter Ring sees far, the Vein Pit is thickest
  • Residue crystal grows. On any air face touching budding residue, buds advance small → medium → large (never in water), at a twenty-fifth of amethyst's pace and never inside the Filter Ring. A large bud does not turn into a block of residue by itself. Each step makes a small sound and a puff of purple motes
  • The Kinetic Seizure really runs. Its shaft turns at 16 RPM without stopping, purple motes leak from it, and within 12 blocks a low grinding loop plays. Every five seconds there is an 8% chance that the nearest player within six blocks catches four seconds of Residue Sickness (creative and spectator players excepted). Its tooltip now reads 'Unstable. Do not stand close.' and '16 RPM · 256 SU', and it glows faintly
  • Use the Survey Map and a map of the city appears in the middle of the screen for ten seconds: the diamond, the Filter Ring, the Vein Pit, the Condenser Reach to the east, and, inside the city, an arrow for where you stand and which way you face. Sneak to close it. Used outside the city it says the survey plate is inert here
  • In 4.2.0 not a single particle was drawn (the falling-debris warning, the condenser coil's drips, the filter lamp's sparks): the client-side registration was missing. All three are visible from 4.2.1, and blue-green sparks now drift around filter lamps
  • The Ashen Gateway answers a right-click at last: on the gateway it takes you to the city's centre, and inside the city it brings you back where you were. It can also be crafted now: 4 precast concrete + 4 residue crystals + 1 ender pearl

Balance changes (3)

  • Time before Residue Sickness sets in bare-faced: 8 seconds → 10 seconds (the 40 seconds with a filter mask is unchanged; the mask's 240 durability has been in place since 4.2.0)
  • Minimum interval between falling debris: 60 seconds → 80 seconds (the 120-second maximum is unchanged)
  • Range at which a Pulse Hound's howl is triggered: 24 blocks → 20 blocks

Warnings (5)
