V1.3

The Washitsu Architectural Masterpiece — thirty pieces of a Japanese room, and a factory that runs itself

The Washitsu Architectural Masterpiece — thirty pieces of a Japanese room, and a factory that runs itself

Thirty pieces for a Japanese room, and ten facilities in the second industrial update. Tatami and engawa, shoji and fusuma, ranma and byobu, a chabudai and a tansu, a kakejiku, an incense burner, lanterns, a shishi-odoshi, a furin and a go board. For the factory: a transceiver that sends items through walls, a miner that digs with your tool, a bus that draws straight from the vault, and more. Every new sound is synthesised from scratch.

Highlights (10)

  • Tatami: an igusa mat covers 2 blocks with one of 4 borders, and standing in a room of 8 or more joined cells makes hunger drain half as fast. Borderless checkered tatami laid together form a checkerboard.
  • Shoji and fusuma: an empty hand or a redstone signal slides them about one block open, and two side by side slide past each other. Four fusuma in a row make one gold-leaf landscape.
  • Kamoi and nageshi lintels: a carved ranma fits on top, and a kakejiku, a sword stand, a noren, a sudare or a furin hangs from them. The noren parts on the side you walk through; up to 3 sudare roll as one.
  • Furniture: sit on a zabuton or a zaisu, set out up to 4 things on a chabudai, and keep 27 stacks in a tansu. Whisk and drink matcha for full health and Haste II for 3 minutes (one bowl every 5 minutes).
  • Tokonoma and garden: the kakejiku's painting follows the seasons, a new one every 8 in-game days, and the yukimi-toro gathers snow in winter. The shishi-odoshi strikes its stone every 4.4 seconds; the furin rings in the wind.
  • The OUKA Matter Transceiver: link it with a Resonance Linker and it sends up to a stack and a bucket every tick, through walls, up to 32 blocks away, from one sender to as many as four receivers. No power needed.
  • The Resonant Miner digs with the pickaxe or shovel you give it and, at normal speed, takes as long as that tool would take you. Silk Touch and Fortune work too. An area of up to 5×5, up to 16 deep.
  • The Quantum Vault I/O Bus draws the items you choose (up to 9 kinds) from the vault, up to 256 a tick, and leaves the count you set inside. An OUKA conveyor takes only 1 a tick.
  • The Hyper-Impulse Hopper moves 64 items a tick into a chest or pipe (1 onto an OUKA conveyor). The Cobblestone Synthesizer never uses up the bucket of lava and the bucket of water you give it, and, on grid power, makes cobblestone, gravel, sand or ore sand.
  • Also: a 3×3 crafter, a pump and pipes that move 8 buckets of fluid a tick, a grinder that stores experience in capsules, a terminal that opens the vault even from another dimension, and an accelerator pylon that speeds OUKA machines up to ×10.

Warnings (3)

  • This release fixes two sounds that never played in V1.0.1, V1.1.0 or V1.2.0: the OUKA Beacon terminal's slider tick and the Ouka Caster selector's confirm sound. If you are on an earlier version, please replace it.
  • You can also install it from Corvus Store: the first time, press Get in Corvus Store; after that, Corvus Store replaces it with newer versions by itself. If you have turned Automatic Updates off, press Update instead. (To install by hand, use the OUKA page.)
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 (bundled in the zip) and Java 25.

V1.2

The Heavy Industrial & Kinematics Epoch — heavy industry you can watch at work

The Heavy Industrial & Kinematics Epoch — heavy industry you can watch at work

Twenty pieces of heavy industry: a power grid and power stations, a vast vault, conveyor and drone logistics, smelting and processing, mining and automatic harvesting, and field tools with a hazard exoskeleton. No machine is a box where numbers go up: arms, hammers, drills and rotors really move, and each machine sounds as hard as it works. This release also fixes V1.1.0's two defects.

Highlights (10)

  • A power grid: Tesla pylons link without wires within 64 blocks, and arcs run from them to the machines. No cables.
  • Power stations and storage: a fission core (up to 2 MW; it scrams at 800 °C and never explodes), a solar tower that keeps running at night on stored heat, and a 400 MJ superconducting battery.
  • The Quantum-Compression Vault: build the 5×5×7 shell and it holds up to 2,147,483,647 items, searchable by name or by "@mod".
  • Maglev conveyors carry 20 items a second. Their shape follows their neighbours, you see every item riding them, nothing falls off, and a blocked line queues. Plus a splitter and a six-way sorter.
  • Logistics drones: link two ports with a route card and a drone ferries loads between them — even through unloaded areas, arriving after the flight time.
  • Smelting and processing: an arc foundry that smelts 32 ores at sixteen times furnace speed, a hydraulic crusher for double dusts and plates, an assembly line whose three arms weld, and a centrifuge that draws out titanium, lithium and helium-3.
  • Mining and harvesting: a bore that digs ore and crude oil without end and without breaking bedrock, a desalinator that pumps 200 buckets a second, a timber mill that fells, planks and replants, and a culture vat.
  • The Omni-Wrench (turn machines round, open and close exits, overclock up to ×2.50) and the Demolition Laser (a machine and everything in it back in your inventory in one second).
  • The Holographic Blueprint: record up to 16 blocks a side, check the life-size hologram, then build it from the items you carry.
  • The Hazard Exoskeleton: shocks, heat and falls no longer hurt, you can swim in lava, and you mine three times as fast.

Warnings (3)

  • This release fixes V1.1.0's two defects (a mined Block of Ouka Alloy dropped nothing; the shelter's loot table). If you are on V1.1.0, please replace it.
  • You can also install it from Corvus: the first time, press Download in Corvus; after that, Corvus replaces it with newer versions. (To install by hand, use the OUKA page.)
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 (bundled in the zip) and Java 25.

V1.1

The Operating Shelter — bedrock for the operator alone

The Operating Shelter — bedrock for the operator alone

A block that only an operator can place or break. No explosion, no mob and no creative-mode break touches it. This release also adds seventeen sounds made from nothing, puts every recipe in the recipe book from the start, and fixes the crash in V1.0.0.

Highlights (10)

  • The Operating Shelter Block: only a player with operator permission can place or break it. Nobody else can, in any game mode.
  • No explosion gets through — Alpha's nuclear weapons, its other warheads and rockets, and TNT alike.
  • An ordinary player cannot break it in creative either. Creative's instant break ignores hardness, so that path is closed separately.
  • The wither, the ender dragon, an enderman carrying it away, a piston — none of them touch it. It sits in all eleven tags vanilla puts bedrock in.
  • An operator mines it normally in survival and gets it back (a diamond pickaxe or better).
  • When anyone else touches it, a hexagonal energy lattice snaps across the block's faces, with a metallic repulsion and sparks.
  • Seventeen sounds, every one made from nothing. No sound from another game, no sample library, no recording.
  • Twelve colours are twelve semitones: each circle colour sounds at its own pitch, so you can hear which one flew.
  • Every recipe is in the recipe book from the start. In V1.0.0 the unlock condition was too strict and nothing appeared.
  • The shelter is crafted from 4 obsidian, 4 netherite ingots and 1 block of Ouka alloy, for 2.

Warnings (3)

  • If you are on V1.0.0, please replace it. V1.0.0 crashes the game the moment a circle projectile comes into view, when "Improved Transparency" is on in Video Settings.
  • Install from the OUKA page. Corvus shows OUKA's changelog but does not install OUKA.
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 (bundled in the zip) and Java 25.

V1.0

The Apex Artifact — an altar, and danmaku in twelve colours

The Apex Artifact — an altar, and danmaku in twelve colours

OUKA's first release: a mod for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric), separate from Alpha and Cherry. Raise an altar on its pedestal and every player within 1,000 blocks receives its effects, and two items fire circle projectiles in twelve colours.

Highlights (10)

  • The OUKA Beacon: a 3×3×3 structure. Breaking any part removes all 27 and drops one beacon carrying its tuning.
  • A 328-block pedestal of netherite, diamond, emerald or Ouka alloy in eight layers. It wakes only when all 328 are there.
  • A 1,000-block aura that reaches players even while the beacon's own area is unloaded. Spectators receive nothing.
  • Ten effects tuned steplessly from 0.00 to 20.00: speed, mining, attack speed, strength, jump, safe fall, absorption, regeneration, food, resistance and night vision.
  • The constant effects of an enchanted golden apple, for as long as the aura reaches you — they return after death while the altar stands.
  • A frosted-glass terminal: right-click to open, then drag, or use the wheel in steps of 0.1, or 0.01 with Shift. Three presets.
  • Only the player who placed it, or an operator, may change it, and only from within 8 blocks. Everyone else sees it read-only.
  • Twelve colours of circle, each with its own effect: a freeze, a pull, a line that pierces walls, a split into two homing circles, and one that cycles the other eleven.
  • Five trajectories: straight, spiral, converging twelve onto the point you look at, an orbital guard of eight, and homing.
  • The shockwave on impact: a flat ring erases the enemies it crosses. Bosses take 20% of their maximum health. Allies and blocks are untouched.

Warnings (3)

  • One colour flashes the screen white on impact (Celestial White). It does not flash if Minecraft's "Hide Lightning Flashes" setting is on.
  • Install from the OUKA page. Corvus shows OUKA's changelog but does not install OUKA.
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0+26.3 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 (bundled in the zip) and Java 25.

V1.2

Guns, Moon life, Lunaris Prime and Mars

Guns, Moon life, Lunaris Prime and Mars

Cherry's biggest update: five next-generation guns with first-person animation and recoil, three kinds of life on the Moon, the boss Lunaris Prime sealed in a ruin far out on the Moon, the red planet Mars with its cold and iron dust storms, a navigation console that flies the Apex-1 to any of three worlds, and the Advanced Cherry Fabricator, which makes every gun, magazine and vehicle part.

Highlights (8)

  • Five guns: the CR-14 bullpup rifle, the CP-9 sub-machine gun, the CS-50 rail sniper, the CX-12 magpulse shotgun and the C-Pistol. Right-click aims and softly blurs what lies nearer and farther than your aim, R reloads (in survival with one of that gun's magazines) and F inspects; with recoil, muzzle flash, casings, holographic sights, the CS-50's 6× thermal scope and the C-Pistol's laser sight.
  • Life on the Moon: the Selenite Stalker hides under the regolith, leaps at anyone who walks without sneaking and bites holes in pressure suits; the Void Drifter's EMP blanks your guns' sights and ammo readouts; the Regolith Devourer slams the ground to fling you high into the sky and hurls molten meteors.
  • Lunaris Prime: beyond 8,000 blocks on the Moon a giant crater holds the Astraea Shackle (/locate finds it). An Astral Catalyst in its seal console wakes the boss; the sky turns crimson, and at half health its armour flies off for a second, faster phase.
  • Its relics: the Coreshard of Lunaris, the Graviton Siphon (its shot opens a black hole) and the Crown of Selene.
  • Mars: press launch on the pad to open the Apex-1's Stellar Navigation console and pick Earth, the Moon or Mars. Mars has 0.38 g and unbreathable air at -60 C: the pressure suit keeps the air out, and a Thermal Lining sewn in at the smithing table keeps the cold out.
  • Iron dust storms blow over Mars about every 20 minutes: the fog closes to a few blocks and noise crosses Cherry's HUDs.
  • Mars's resources: Martian Ferrite and Perchlorate Energy Salt. Perchlorate Propellant is rocket fuel worth three canisters.
  • The Advanced Cherry Fabricator: pick a recipe and fill its six inputs; it makes the guns, their magazines, gun parts, fuel and every vehicle, building the Apex-1 from boosters and the Dreadnought from railgun barrels that it mass-produces from helium-3 and regolith cores; hoppers can feed it.

Warnings (1)

  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 or newer and Java 25.

V1.1

Sounds come back after an audio device change

Sounds come back after an audio device change

When the audio device changes (earphones plugged in or out, or a device chosen in the options), the game restarts its sound engine and drops every looping sound. In 1.1.0 to 1.1.3, Cherry's aircraft engines, afterburner, missile motors and lock tones, and the pressure suit's breathing, then stayed silent until you boarded again or put the helmet back on. They now start again by themselves.

Highlights (2)

  • After an audio device change, the Stratos-900 and Aegis-X engines, the Aegis-X afterburner, missile motors and the lock tones start again by themselves within a moment.
  • On the Moon, the pressure suit's breathing does the same.

Warnings (1)

  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 or newer and Java 25.

Easy controls, and the railgun hits

Flying and sailing are rebuilt so that anyone can do it. The view is no longer locked: the aircraft fly where you look, and the ships answer to W, A, S and D like a boat. The Dreadnought's railgun now strikes what it hits; in 1.1.0 to 1.1.2 its shells damaged nothing.

Highlights (11)

  • Aircraft fly where you look: turn your head and the Stratos-900 or the Aegis-X banks, turns, climbs or dives towards it by herself. W and S set the throttle; left-click fires the Aegis-X's missiles at the target the radar picks by itself.
  • Flaps, landing gear, brakes, reverse thrust, the afterburner, the canopy and the arresting hook now work by themselves, and a short guide on the screen shows the controls when you board.
  • The view is yours: it no longer locks to first person or tilts with the aircraft, F5 works in flight, and holding Left Alt lets you look around while she keeps her course.
  • Ships: standing at a station shows the key that takes it. At the wheel, W and S drive the engine (astern below zero), A and D steer, and letting go centres the wheel and holds her heading; Sneak leaves. The ferry's doors are on Y.
  • With nobody at the gunnery console, the Dreadnought's helmsman fires the guns from the wheel: left-click for a salvo, right-click for the VLS.
  • Fixed from 1.1.2: the rudder turned the ship the opposite way to its keys, astern gave no thrust, a ship could not be launched from the shore (she now floats with her stern just past the water you aim at), and a ship could not be picked back up in survival.
  • The railgun's shells burst on the first block, creature, ship or aircraft in their path and damage what lies within 4 m; when a salvo lands on one target, all six shells count. Look at any part of a hull and both turrets train on that point.
  • The gun spares the ship that fired it and her crew, and it never breaks blocks. No ship or aircraft is sunk or brought down by a single salvo, and manning the guns no longer loads distant chunks.
  • A death message from one of Cherry's weapons no longer shows an untranslated key when the attacker holds a renamed item.
  • The Aegis-X's missile count at the bottom left of the flight display is no longer cut off by the bottom of the screen. With the automatic GUI scale this happened at many screen sizes, among them 720p, 1440p, 4K and the game's default window.
  • Chart waypoints are accepted only within the chart's widest view, about 1.4 km from the ship, which also protects servers from malformed waypoint requests.

Warnings (3)

  • The controls have changed: 1.1.2's flight keys (R, U, Y, K, Z, N, M, J and the arrow keys) and helm keys (, . = - ; ') are gone, and any of them you rebound are no longer used.
  • Railgun shells now hurt whatever they strike, including other players' ships and aircraft. The crew member directing the guns is credited with the damage.
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 or newer and Java 25.

The ships, finished: real models, working stations and the ferry's doors

The Leviathan and the Dreadnought get their real models and interiors, and every station aboard now works from the game. In 1.1.0 and 1.1.1 the railgun, the VLS and the chart table could not actually be used; now they can.

Highlights (6)

  • Real ships: hulls, decks and interiors are fully modelled — the ferry's vehicle deck, lounge, observation deck and bridge, and the destroyer's bridge, CIC and hangar — with windows you can see through that light up at night. Every deck you see is a deck you can walk on.
  • Stations: stand at the wheel, the chart table, or the destroyer's gunnery or sonar station and press the station key (H by default) to take it. The chart table opens a chart you click to lay a route on; the new sonar station shows contacts on a sweeping scope.
  • The Dreadnought now carries two triple 406 mm railgun turrets. Both train on the point you look at and fire one salvo on a left click, with muzzle flashes, blast rings, tracers and recoiling barrels. A right click fires the VLS at the radar-locked target once its hatch is open, venting white smoke.
  • The Leviathan's bow visor and bow and stern ramps: stopped at the wheel, press the doors key (' by default). The visor rises before the ramp lowers, the engine stays locked until everything is shut again, and you can walk up the lowered ramps.
  • Wake and bubbles: a ship under way throws a bow wave at the Kelvin angle and leaves a wake behind her, and a turning screw churns bubbles.
  • The helm keys no longer clash with Minecraft's own keys: , and . turn the wheel, = and - set the engine, and ; holds the heading. The helm instruments now sit above the hotbar and health bar instead of on top of them.

Warnings (2)

  • The helm keys have new names, so a binding you changed for them in 1.1.0 or 1.1.1 is not carried over. Set it again.
  • Requires Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer, Fabric API 0.161.0 or newer, GeckoLib 5.5.6 or newer and Java 25.

The Aerospace & Naval Update — into the air and out to sea

Cherry gains aircraft, airports and ships. You can walk about on a flying aircraft or a ship under way.

Highlights (6)

  • Two aircraft: the Cherry Stratos-900 passenger and freight ferry and the Cherry Aegis-X stealth fighter, with a first-person cockpit, artificial horizon and reticle, retracting gear, flaps and afterburner.
  • Airport fittings: PAPI lights that change colour with your approach angle, a catapult to launch an aircraft, an arresting wire that catches a lowered tail hook, and a control radar that shows the aircraft around it.
  • Two ships: the 48 m Cherry Leviathan-Class ocean car ferry and the 64 m Cherry Dreadnought-Class guided missile destroyer. Put one on the water, take the wheel to steer, or lay a route at the chart table and she will sail it, keeping clear of shallows.
  • Only the Dreadnought-Class is armed: a 406 mm railgun turret that trains and elevates independently and rocks the hull when it fires, and a VLS that launches only once its hatch is fully open. The car ferry carries no armament.
  • Deck walking: run and jump about on a moving aircraft or ship. The deck under you carries you with it.
  • A ship goes back into your hand when she is stopped and nobody is on her deck. She remembers the damage she took, so putting her back on the water does not mend her.

Warnings (1)

Alpha is retired: this update switches it off

Alpha is retired: this update switches it off

This is the last Alpha release. It switches Alpha off. The 13 mods are replaced by placeholders that add nothing to the game, and nothing new has been added. Blocks that Alpha added turn into empty air, and once a world has been saved that way they do not come back. Back up your worlds before you update.

Highlights (3)

  • Alpha is switched off. The 13 mods are replaced by placeholders that add nothing to the game (Cherry, OUKA and Aureum are not touched)
  • Only the overworld's height limit (Y up to 1023) stays, so that nothing you built above Y=319 with ordinary blocks is deleted
  • Corvus removes the old jars, places the placeholders and keeps a backup of the files it replaced. If you installed by hand, or run a server, delete the old jars as the pack's README says

Warnings (3)

  • Back up your worlds first. Blocks that Alpha added turn into empty air when a world is opened without Alpha, and once it has been saved that way they are gone for good. Putting Alpha back does not restore them (checked on a dedicated server). Alpha's items and creatures are expected to go the same way (not tested)
  • A server and its players must move together: a player who still has Alpha cannot join a server that no longer does, and the other way round
  • If "Install updates automatically" is on in Corvus, this update is applied without asking. Corvus's backup covers the mods folder, not your worlds

Not yet implemented/confirmed at this point (1)

  • Everything else Alpha had on this site has been removed: every earlier download, its recipes, and its install guide, gates, features, roadmap and known-issues pages. This entry is all that remains of its changelog.

1.0

First release — memory savings that stay out of chunk rendering

First release — memory savings that stay out of chunk rendering

A separate optimisation mod for Minecraft 26.2 (Fabric), apart from Alpha. It works on game logic, memory and the server tick, never touches chunk rendering, and so runs beside Sodium and the Alpha pack. For higher FPS, install Sodium.

Highlights (4)

  • Shared block-state caches and a lighter thread-contention detector: 6.3 MB less resident heap with 2,209 chunks loaded (two independent measurements agree).
  • An expiry time for the structure template cache: 17.2 MB less heap while generating new land (two measurements agree). Unsaved templates in a structure block are never dropped.
  • Each optimisation can be switched off on its own in config/aureum.json, and a switched-off item leaves vanilla code untouched.
  • /aureum (needs permission) shows what is enabled and how much is being shared.

Warnings (3)

  • Tick time (MSPT) is unchanged within measurement noise; Aureum makes no speed claim.
  • The two client-side settings (a particle limit and a block-entity render distance cap) are off by default, and their effect has not been measured.
  • Requires Fabric Loader 0.19.3 or later, Fabric API, and Java 25 or later.
