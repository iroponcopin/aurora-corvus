Download the Alpha mod pack (all 13 mods) directly from this page. The ZIP contains all 13 mod jars, the Sapporo module's data file, the install manuals, the README, and the resource pack.

Download

For server admins: extract the ZIP's jar files straight into your mods folder. The Sapporo mod additionally needs its bundled sapporo_mode_a.spro copied into the server's config/sorakaze_sapporo/data/ folder (see "How to install" below).

The Sapporo mod (sorakaze-sapporo) ships for the first time in V2.2.0. It's a city-generation world based on the real terrain and buildings of Sapporo, Japan (building heights are currently estimated pending real 3D building data; will be updated once that's available).

What's new in V4.3.8

From the Filter Ring's north gate, a <b>runway</b> the Ashen Sparrow can put down on now runs out. At the far end of the North Taxi stand the Ashen Airfield, Hangar One and the Avgas Bunker. Alongside it, the doors have been rebuilt. The flicker fix used until now (floating the face by 0.002 px) was measured, on this Mac's rendering, to <b>swap over again from 12 to 16 blocks away</b>, so the shapes of 12 doors and 2 locks were reassembled with no hidden faces. On top of that, <b>16 new doors</b> are added from the design drawings, to the same standard as the guns. View the changelog →

Aureum (lightweight optimisation mod, separate download)

Aureum is a separate, independent mod from the Alpha pack. It optimises game logic, memory, and server tick cost, and deliberately never touches the chunk-render pipeline. It is not a replacement for Sodium — it optimises the side Sodium does not touch, so it is safe to run alongside it. If you want higher FPS, install Sodium separately.
