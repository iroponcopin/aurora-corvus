Sorakazekarasu Server — Unofficial Mod Reference

Glimpse Alpha Wiki

This is an unofficial reference site for Glimpse Alpha (formerly known as Sorakaze), the Fabric mod group running on the Sorakazekarasu server — covering its recipes, changelog, and install instructions. We currently run 10 mods (guns, rail, building materials, boss, sky, survival, vehicles, power, Backrooms, and Planarcadia) together on Minecraft 26.2.

This site does not distribute the mods themselves (the jar files). What's here is just "reading material" — recipe tables, changelogs, install instructions, and the like. Please get the mod files directly from your server admin.

New feature V2.1.0 2026-08-22

Latest update: Backrooms Levels 3–8 become real, endless levels; a server-admin app on the in-game PC; boss summon recipes reach the recipe book

The largest change this round is Backrooms Levels 3 through 8. They used to be 128-block demo islands; they are now endless levels you can actually walk into and explore. The in-game PC in the Building & Decor mod gained a server-administration app, and in the Boss mod the summoning items' recipes now appear in the in-game recipe book. The three data-driven guns each have their own in-hand model at last.

  • Only four mods actually changed: Backrooms, Building & Decor, Boss and Guns. The other six (Planarcadia, Power, Rail, Sky, Survival, Vehicles) were bumped to 2.1.0 only to keep the set consistent — their jars differ from the 2.0.0 ones in exactly one entry, the version string in fabric.mod.json. Not a byte of their content changed.
  • Backrooms Levels 3 to 8 went from 128-block demo islands to real, endless levels. None of them has an edge any more; all of them can be walked and explored.
  • Level 3, the Electrical Station. The current is live and touching a panel is dangerous. A Hound patrols, and a Smiler waits in the underground cable room.
  • Level 4, the Empty Offices. Rated safe — but 3% of the windows are watching you, and they look exactly like the rest. Sanity slowly recovers here. The almond water is in the break room.

About this project

It started out under the name "Sorakaze," adding three mods — guns, rail, and building materials — on top of an otherwise vanilla survival experience. From there it kept expanding with craftable items, and was renamed "Glimpse Alpha" in v1.8.0. It's now a 10-mod pack that includes bosses, a new dimension, survival features, vehicles, and power infrastructure.

The consistent policy has been to never replace or remove vanilla content — things like bows and minecarts remain exactly as they were. No real-world firearm, company, or trademark names are used.
