Sorakazekarasu Server — Unofficial Mod Reference

Glimpse Alpha Wiki

This is an unofficial reference site for Glimpse Alpha (formerly known as Sorakaze), the Fabric mod group running on the Sorakazekarasu server — covering its recipes, changelog, and install instructions. We currently run 11 mods (guns, rail, building materials, boss, sky, survival, vehicles, power, Backrooms, and Planarcadia) together on Minecraft 26.2.

As of V2.2.0, this site distributes the mod files (jars) directly via the "Download" page. It also has "reading material" — recipe tables, changelogs, install instructions, and the like. If you need a version older than V2.2.0, please get it directly from your server admin.

New feature V2.2.0 2026-08-23

Latest update: 52 new glass lanterns, a real Backrooms lobby, overworld raids, turrets, and a real reconstruction of Sapporo

This round adds an 11th mod, Sapporo — a dimension recreating the real city of Sapporo, Japan, from real survey data. Backrooms Level 1, "The Lobby", is now a real explorable space rather than a demo room, with a glass objective panel shown on the left of the screen at all times. Village raids can now trigger on pure chance without Bad Omen, turrets let you mount guns and bows, video now plays up to 4K and stops automatically when you walk away, the Sparxie staff gained a ranged attack alongside its melee one, the minigun and rocket launcher were rebalanced, a flyable camera drone and its own phone app arrived, and the Building & Decor mod gained 52 new glass lanterns. The Amethyst Gate now opens with plain vanilla amethyst blocks too.

  • The Sapporo mod (sorakaze_sapporo) has finally shipped. It's a dimension recreating the real city of Sapporo, Japan, from real geographic data. Building footprints and terrain are both 100% real survey data, but most building heights are currently a documented per-district statistical estimate — an honest gap while the more detailed 3D building dataset (Japan's PLATEAU) is still being obtained. Heights will be refined once that data is in hand.
  • Backrooms Level 1, "The Lobby", is now a real, walkable, explorable space rather than a small demo room. You reach it through a rift that generates naturally near an End City.
  • A glass objective panel is now shown on the left side of the screen at all times, showing your current level, sanity, and where to head next.
  • Inside the Backrooms, sanity now replaces thirst as the stat you're managing. Entities you meet there take no damage from ordinary weapons and cannot be defeated — this is a place for evasion, not combat.

About this project

It started out under the name "Sorakaze," adding three mods — guns, rail, and building materials — on top of an otherwise vanilla survival experience. From there it kept expanding with craftable items, and was renamed "Glimpse Alpha" in v1.8.0. It's now a 11-mod pack that includes bosses, a new dimension, survival features, vehicles, and power infrastructure.

The consistent policy has been to never replace or remove vanilla content — things like bows and minecarts remain exactly as they were. No real-world firearm, company, or trademark names are used.
