Sorakazekarasu Server — Unofficial Mod Reference

Glimpse Alpha Wiki

This is an unofficial reference site for Glimpse Alpha (formerly known as Sorakaze), the Fabric mod group running on the Sorakazekarasu server — covering its recipes, changelog, and install instructions. We currently run 9 mods (guns, rail, building materials, boss, sky, survival, vehicles, power, and Backrooms) together on Minecraft 26.2.

This site does not distribute the mods themselves (the jar files). What's here is just "reading material" — recipe tables, changelogs, install instructions, and the like. Please get the mod files directly from your server admin.

New feature v1.9.0 2026-08-11

Latest update: New Mod "Backrooms" Joins the Pack, Wither Storm Overhauled

The 9th mod, "Backrooms," has joined the pack — a sanity-driven exploration experience reached through rifts that spawn near End Cities. The boss mod's Wither Storm has been completely overhauled with a new look, growth stages, and a new status effect, plus new guaranteed drops. The other 6 mods (building & decor, guns, power, sky, rail, vehicles) are unchanged.

  • The 9th mod, "Backrooms" (sorakaze_backrooms), has joined the pack. Every time an End City generates, exactly one rift (an altar) spawns nearby in a safe spot — one per City (existing End Cities don't get one).
  • Features a sanity system. It maxes out at 100 and drains to zero in about 18 minutes if you do nothing. Drinking the restorative item found in chests recovers 35. Roughly every 30 seconds there's a 12% chance of a "blackout," and things start to change once sanity drops below 20.
  • You can't place blocks inside the maze, and the walls, floor, and ceiling — including the chests — can't be destroyed at all. Other dimensions are unaffected.
  • The entity "Hollow Listener" roams the maze (40 HP, 6 attack damage. It freezes while you're looking at it and moves again once you look away). Up to 3 can be present at once.

About this project

It started out under the name "Sorakaze," adding three mods — guns, rail, and building materials — on top of an otherwise vanilla survival experience. From there it kept expanding with craftable items, and was renamed "Glimpse Alpha" in v1.8.0. It's now a 9-mod pack that includes bosses, a new dimension, survival features, vehicles, and power infrastructure.

The consistent policy has been to never replace or remove vanilla content — things like bows and minecarts remain exactly as they were. No real-world firearm, company, or trademark names are used.
