Known Issues

A summary of small bugs currently known about, plus features that haven't been confirmed on real hardware yet due to constraints in the development environment. Serious bugs get a fixed version distributed as soon as they're found, as noted in the changelog.

Currently known minor bugs

Known issues that affect display or appearance without breaking functionality.

Intentionally unaddressed items

Not bugs — these are being considered as intentionally deferred, pending a design decision.

Dedicated attack animations for the Kraken and Titan

These two colossal bosses do show a particle warning before their signature moves, but don't yet have dedicated body-movement attack animations. This is an intentionally deferred item, pending a decision from the server admin on whether it gets addressed.

Recent changes not yet confirmed on real hardware

This mod pack is built in an environment where the developers cannot directly operate a Minecraft client. Server startup and data integrity are verified mechanically, but appearance, sound, and how things feel to actually play can only be confirmed through feedback from real players. The items below are recent updates that haven't been confirmed on real hardware yet. If you notice anything off, please let your server admin know.

  • Appearance of the water pipes and gas mask: The appearance and hitboxes reworked in v1.8.5 haven't been confirmed on an actual screen yet.
  • Feel of the enhanced beacon GUI: The usability of the ability-selection screen for the α/β enhanced beacons hasn't been confirmed.
  • Hostile mob spawn density (Overworld): Whether the spawn density adjusted in v1.8.5 feels right in actual play hasn't been verified.
  • New icons for warheads and phone apps: The icons added in v1.8.6 for the missile and nuclear warheads, along with the phone apps' new screen designs, haven't been confirmed on the actual in-game screen yet.
  • Behavior of the security camera, elevator, and other devices after electrification: For the 5 devices that started requiring power in v1.8.3 (security camera, elevator, escalator, automatic door, ATM), actual behavior hasn't yet been confirmed in either the powered or unpowered state.
  • How redstone electrification (v1.8.1+) feels to play with: How much automated farms and stations end up shut down, and how much work it takes to rewire them, won't be clear without actually playing it.
