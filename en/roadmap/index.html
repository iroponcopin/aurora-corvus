Roadmap

Roadmap

An overview of what's currently planned for upcoming updates, based on the development team's internal order documents.

The content on this page has not yet been implemented or released. It's an introduction based on internal planning notes, and the actual content, timing, and specifications that ship may change or be cancelled. Anything finalized will show up on the "Changelog" page.

Planned V2

V2 Update (Concept & Spec Finalized, Implementation Still Ahead)

Building Materials Mod: Video App Bug Fixes & Device Redesign

  • Planned fix so that the video app's play, pause, and fast-forward/rewind controls work correctly (top-priority bug fix).
  • Planned fix for a bug where audio keeps playing after leaving the server or moving to another world.
  • Planned fix for a bug where audio plays but no video is shown (screen stays black).
  • Concept to completely redesign the appearance of the iPhone, MacBook, and battery pack based on new design proposals.

Water Systems Overhaul & Living Infrastructure

  • Concept to move away from vanilla water flow behavior toward a system where water physically and naturally flows from high ground to low ground (eliminating endless horizontal spread on flat ground, with water instead pooling in depressions).
  • Planned addition of a full water infrastructure set — pipes, pumps, faucets, and more — connecting water sources through to taps.
  • Water bottle capacity planned to quadruple. Thirst is expected to deplete slightly faster than hunger, and once thirst has persisted for 10 minutes it would start stacking damage — potentially fatal — unless a regeneration effect is active.
  • Planned automatic backup feature letting admins configure interval, retention count, and destination via a UI (setup instructions also planned for this site).

Firearms Mod: Balance Adjustments & Sound Overhaul

  • Under consideration: Minigun performance +50%, Assault Rifle +75%, Pistol power x1.5, SMG damage halved in exchange for +30% magazine capacity, Rocket Launcher damage and blast power both x3, and Turret damage x4 with durability +70%.
  • Concept to scale nuclear warheads up to destroying as many as 10 million blocks, and to triple both the blast power and radius of missile warheads (the nuclear warhead's own power is expected to stay based on its current value — a direct hit of 67,200 as of v1.8.8 — as the baseline).
  • Also planned: a fix for the Rocket Launcher's body model blocking the player's view while using the scope.
  • Concept to completely remake the gunfire sounds (reportedly not reusing audio from any specific existing game as-is, but producing new sounds in a similar style).
  • Concept to add 3 new missile launcher designs, all of which players would be able to pilot themselves.

Boss Mod: 4 New Bosses & Existing Boss Adjustments

  • Planned addition of 4 new bosses: "Volcanic Golem King" (HP 600), "Abyssal Kraken Warden" (HP 800), "Prismatic Ice Phoenix Dragon" (HP 750), and "Witherstorm Demon Overlord" (HP 1200, whose summoning condition is defeating the above three). All are envisioned with their own summoning items and phase-based fights.
  • Under consideration for the Plague Doctor: a 20-second grace period between taking damage and the Wither Storm summon — defeating it within 20 seconds (or in one hit) would prevent the summon, but would also forfeit any drops.
  • Concept for the Grim Reaper boss: on defeat, a low chance to drop its signature weapon, the "Soul-Reaping Scythe"; killing enemies with that scythe would have a low chance of yielding a spawn egg. The boss itself is also planned to get +15% strength and 3 new attack patterns.
  • Concept to further strengthen the Wither Storm and add a second form (phase) transition.

Survival & Power: Contamination System and New Carrying Actions

  • Nuclear warhead impact sites are expected to enter a "contaminated" state for 20 Minecraft days. Concept for this to be neutralized only by wearing both a gas mask and protective suit.
  • Under consideration: running a nuclear power generator continuously would contaminate the surrounding air, affecting players' health if they stay nearby too long without protection. A new "Air Purifier" block is also planned, which would cleanse contamination in its area while running (with support for multiple units operating at once).
  • Planned feature to let players pick up and carry small mobs, such as villagers and chickens, by hand.
  • Also under consideration: a two-handed carry action for chests, letting them be moved without breaking (contents would be preserved; double chests are envisioned as requiring two players to carry).
  • Planned addition of a new item (tentative name) with 32 durability and 200 attack power. A ranged attack using the same mechanism as the Magic Wand is under consideration.

Bank UI, Backrooms, and Site Integration

  • Planned increase of the ATM/MineBank deposit cap to 10 million Emeralds. Concept to apply "Glimpse Alpha Glass" styling to the ATM screen and overhaul the UI as a whole.
  • Planned addition of a new "MineBank UI" (reportedly with a design proposal featuring 25%/50%/75%/MAX quick-select based on balance, plus slider controls in increments of 1/10/100/1k).
  • Concept to completely redesign the appearance of the Backrooms entities (monsters) based on new reference artwork.
  • Concept to build a system so that once future update details are finalized, they're automatically reflected in this site's changelog (this concerns the operation of this site itself).
