Roadmap

Roadmap

An overview of what's currently planned for upcoming updates, based on the development team's internal order documents.

The content on this page has not yet been implemented or released. It's an introduction based on internal planning notes, and the actual content, timing, and specifications that ship may change or be cancelled. Anything finalized will show up on the "Changelog" page.

Planned v1.9.0

New Area "Escape from the Backrooms" & Wither Storm Integration

New Area: "Escape from the Backrooms"

  • A new dedicated map faithfully recreating the source material.
  • A "Sanity" stat will be introduced while inside the area, recoverable with items like "Almond Water" that drop within it.
  • A special rule inside the area prevents placing or breaking blocks.
  • Loot-filled chests placed throughout the area.
  • Monsters and gimmicks recreating the source material will appear.
  • The entrance is planned to generate randomly inside End Cities.
  • Progress is expected to be shown as text on the left side of the screen at all times.

Wither Storm Integration

  • Plans to integrate the third-party "Wither Storm" mod by bundling it in the mods folder.
  • The Plague Doctor will start summoning Wither Storms.
  • Defeating it is planned to drop a "Universal Harvester α" and a "Firework Doll α" (upgraded versions of the existing Universal Harvester and Firework Doll) as rewards.
  • The Universal Harvester α has durability equivalent to a golden pickaxe and cannot be enchanted.
  • The Firework Doll α is being considered with unlimited uses and a 3-minute cooldown, a different design from the existing Firework Doll's 3-use limit.
Planned v1.9.1

Major Rail Mod Enhancements

Operation automation

  • Planned addition of an auto-departure feature to the train auto-stop block.
  • An "autopilot mode" that lets trains run without a player aboard is under consideration.
  • Added running sound effects.

New cars and equipment

  • Higher-class cars, such as Green Car and Gran Class, are planned.
  • Overhead catenary wires (strung above the tracks) added as a placeable item.
  • Interior and exterior lighting to brighten up nighttime running.
  • Support for coupling minecarts to each other.
  • Fully 3D-modeled vanilla rails.
  • A smartphone-style next-station/departure info UI is planned.
Planned v1.9.3

Nuclear Warhead Energy, Coordinate Display & UI Improvements

New features

  • Plans to let nuclear warheads be used as an energy source for the Power mod (a different use case from the missile/nuclear warheads already added in v1.8.4).
  • An always-on coordinate display UI is planned.

Improvements

  • Improvements to the UI that shows TPS and ping.

Further-out ideas (not yet part of this update)

  • "High-Performance Core": reportedly there's an idea to combine it with a mace at a crafting table to multiply the mace's performance by 20x.
