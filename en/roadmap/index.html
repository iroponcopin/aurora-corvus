Roadmap

Roadmap

This page separates what has already shipped from what is still to be built. Most of the items that used to sit here under "planned" are already in the pack being distributed. The shipped work is gathered first, and what is still to be made comes after it.

Everything on the green "Shipped" card really is in the pack currently being distributed. Everything on the blue cards is not in your game yet. Timing, content and specifications may change, or be dropped. Only what has actually shipped appears on the Changelog page.

Shipped V2.2.0 – V2.5.1

What this page used to list as "planned" — most of it has already shipped

This page itself had gone out of date

  • For a long time this page went on presenting V2.2 as the next release, not yet started. That was not true. Everything from V2.2.0 (2026-08-23) through V2.5.1 (2026-08-28) has already been distributed, and the pack being handed out now is V2.5.1.
  • It also said the Sapporo mod (sorakaze_sapporo) was not bundled yet and had been carried over to V2.2. That was not true either. The Sapporo mod shipped in V2.2.0 as the eleventh mod, and it is in the ZIP being distributed today. The jars you install are one Fabric API plus Alpha's 12.
  • Note also that there is no release numbered V2.3. V2.2.1 was followed by V2.4.0. The numbering simply skips a step; no update is missing.

Written up here as still being planned, but already running

  • Weather and disasters (blizzard, typhoon, earthquake, tsunami, volcano) are all five implemented, and all five are enabled by default. Even on default settings, an earthquake can break perfectly ordinary building materials: stone, cobblestone, sandstone, dirt, sand, gravel and clay.
  • The mechanism that raises prices to between 10× and 100× is enabled by default in the Sky mod and the Planarcadia mod (vanilla villager trading is not affected).
  • The ATM's diamond balance is enabled by default. The point this page said would be "decided from measurements and then published" has in fact already been decided: the rate is fixed at 1 diamond = 4 emeralds and cannot be changed in the config. No interest at all accrues on a diamond balance.
  • Raising the interest ceiling to 10,000,000 emeralds has already been applied as well. Strictly, it is the ceiling on the balance that earns interest, and for the special account alone it is 50,000,000 emeralds.
  • Ten minutes at zero thirst causing escalating damage that can genuinely kill you outside Peaceful, petrol for vehicles other than trains, and mask durability with its one-time revival are all running by default too. Mask durability is 814, not the "around 800" originally stated, and the revival takes 320 seconds.
  • The exact figures above, and the config key for each of them, are collected under "Previously Undocumented Mechanics" on the Feature Guide page. That list was written up by checking the mods' own code, one item at a time.

The parts of the V2 plan that shipped

  • The entrance to Planarcadia and the Portal Amethyst Block; four new bosses (600, 800, 750 and 1,200 health) and their summoning rituals; water infrastructure (pipes, pump, tap); the refrigerator and food spoilage; petrol and jerrycans; the air purifier and nuclear-plant air pollution; the hazmat suit — V2.0.0 (2026-08-18).
  • The mechanism that leaves the impact site of a nuclear warhead contaminated for 20 in-game days is implemented as well. Wearing both a gas mask and a hazmat suit negates it; missing either one leaves you taking damage and debuffs.
  • The water bottle now holds four times as much (8 drinks' worth to 32).
  • Villagers, small mobs such as chickens, and chests can be picked up and carried without breaking them. Contents are kept as they were, and a large chest takes two people.
  • The 20-second interval before the Plague Doctor calls a Wither Storm, the Reaper's soul-harvesting scythe and its spawn egg, the Reaper's +15% strength and three added attack patterns, and the Wither Storm's second form with its new destructive attack — all implemented.
  • The bug that stopped the Angel Shield working as a shield is fixed. It really does parry now.
  • The firearm rebalance (minigun, assault rifle, pistol, SMG, rocket launcher, turret), gunshot sounds built from scratch, the fix for the rocket launcher's own body blocking your view while scoped, and the strengthened nuclear and missile warheads — all shipped.
  • Three missile launchers were added — the MK-VII, the VLS Mk-V and the TEL-X3 — and you can board all of them to drive, aim and fire. A separate emplaced launcher, which you place down and fire from the tactical map, exists alongside them.
  • Play, pause and fast-forward/rewind in the video app work. The bug where audio kept playing after leaving the server or changing dimension, and the one where sound played over a black screen, are fixed too.
  • The iPhone and the MacBook have been redrawn, with separate artwork for the inventory icon and for the item held in hand or placed down.
  • The Backrooms entities have been redrawn. They combine a 3D model with pixel art, in a sickly yellow palette, with particles drifting around them. Note that the entities cannot be killed by any attack.
  • Automatic backups are implemented. A server administrator can set the interval, the number kept and the destination from a screen via /glimpsebackup ui; worlds are saved as date-stamped ZIPs, and older ones are tidied away automatically according to the retention count (off, every 30 minutes, 5 kept, by default).
  • The Corvus Bank screen has shipped too, and opening an ATM now shows it by default. It has deposit/withdraw switching, 25%/50%/75%/MAX quick selection, 1/10/100/1k steppers, a fee preview, and confirm/cancel. Note that its tabs switch accounts (personal / shared / special), not currencies.
  • Sparxie's staff (list price 200,000 emeralds), turrets, 52 kinds of glass lantern, overworld raids, the drone and the drone app, and the reconstruction of Sapporo have all shipped as well. For what each individual release contained, see the Changelog page.
Now shipping V3

V3 has started shipping (5 of 50 features)

What is in progress

  • V3 is a major update planning 50 new features, centred on exploration, defence, storage and new terrain. Shipping began with V3.0.0 on 29 August 2026.
  • Rather than landing all at once, it is being built a piece at a time across several releases — so V3 features have arrived in V2-series releases too. The flagship map-targeted missile launcher shipped across V2.4.5 to V2.4.9; the three storage blocks, the rebuilt drone and the Meridian Store shipped in V3.0.0.
  • Five of the fifty have shipped so far. There is no date for the remaining forty-five. Whatever is confirmed as built goes onto the Changelog page as it happens.

Not built yet Undecided

What has not been built (what is left on this page, separate from V3's 50 features)

Rebuilding how water itself flows

  • The plan to replace vanilla's water behaviour has not been implemented. Water still spreads sideways without limit on flat ground, exactly as it did before.
  • The pack does have a "finite water" mechanism of its own, and the water from a tap and the water in a tsunami both run on it. But that is a different block from vanilla water, and vanilla water itself has not been touched at all. There is no finite water you can scoop up in a bucket either.
  • So the water infrastructure and the tsunami do work, but the rebuild of the water mechanic itself is still ahead of us.

The battery's artwork

  • Of the rebuild of the devices' artwork, the iPhone and the MacBook are done. The battery (Power mod) has a 3D model, but no flat inventory icon has been made for it yet, so the same 3D model is shown on every screen.

Figures not published yet

  • This page said the policy for how often each of the five weather types occurs (the average interval) was to publish measured figures, but those figures are still nowhere on this site. Being able to switch each of them off individually in the config is itself implemented.
  • The only figures published so far are the volcanic eruption chance (5% per earthquake) and the crater size (radius 6, depth 4 blocks), which are on the Feature Guide page.
