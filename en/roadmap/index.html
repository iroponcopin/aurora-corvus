Roadmap

Roadmap

An overview of what's currently planned for upcoming updates, based on the development team's internal order documents.

The content on this page has not yet been implemented or released. It's an introduction based on internal planning notes, and the actual content, timing, and specifications that ship may change or be cancelled. Anything finalized will show up on the "Changelog" page.

Planned V2.2

V2.2 (next round — not started, and neither its contents nor its date is settled)

The Sapporo mod (carried over from V2.1.0)

  • The Sapporo mod (sorakaze_sapporo) is not bundled in V2.1.0. It is deferred to V2.2.
  • Until it ships, the number of jars you install does not change. For now it is still 1 Fabric API plus the Alpha pack's 11.

Carried over from V2

  • Everything still listed on the "V2 update" card below — rebuilding how water itself flows, the new weather and disasters, the gun balance and sound work, the money systems, redrawing the Backrooms entities — carries on here.
  • All of it is still at the idea stage. Only what is actually built and shipped appears in the Changelog.
Planned V2

The V2 update (partly shipped in V2.0.0 and V2.1.0 — what remains here has not shipped yet)

Parts of this plan that have already shipped

  • Shipped in V2.0.0 (2026-08-18): the entrance to Planarcadia and the Portal Amethyst Block; four new bosses and their summoning rituals; the Plague Doctor and Reaper adjustments; water infrastructure (pipes, pump, tap); the refrigerator and food spoilage; gasoline and canisters; the air purifier and nuclear-plant air pollution; the hazmat suit; masks that break and repair themselves once.
  • Shipped in V2.1.0 (2026-08-22): Backrooms Levels 3–8 (demo islands to real, endless levels); the server-administration app on the in-game PC, including the automatic-backup settings; boss summon recipes in the recipe book and four discovery advancements; the three data-driven guns' own models.
  • See the Changelog page for the details. What is listed below is what those two rounds have not shipped yet.

Building Materials Mod: Video App Bug Fixes & Device Redesign

  • Planned fix so that the video app's play, pause, and fast-forward/rewind controls work correctly (top-priority bug fix).
  • Planned fix for a bug where audio keeps playing after leaving the server or moving to another world.
  • Planned fix for a bug where audio plays but no video is shown (the screen stays black).
  • Concept to completely redesign the iPhone, MacBook, and battery pack based on new design art — the inventory icon, the in-hand look, and the placed-in-world look are all planned to be redrawn.

Water Rework & Plumbing Infrastructure

  • Concept to drop vanilla water-flow behaviour and switch to water that runs physically downhill — no more endless horizontal spread on flat ground, and water pools in depressions instead.
  • Planned new plumbing set — pipes, a pump, and a tap — so you can run a line from a water source to wherever you need it: the pump draws from a well or river, pipes carry it, and the tap fills your containers.
  • Each plumbing part is planned to get its own 3D model, inventory icon, in-hand appearance, and placed-in-world appearance.
  • The canteen's capacity is planned to be 4× what it is now. Thirst will drain slightly faster than hunger, and staying dehydrated for 10 minutes is expected to escalate damage — fatal unless you have Regeneration active.

Server Operations: Automatic Backups

  • Planned automatic backup feature with an admin UI for the interval, how many backups to keep, and where to put them — so an interval like every 30 minutes can be set from a screen instead of by editing config files.
  • World folders are planned to be snapshotted, compressed, and timestamped, with older backups rotated out according to the retention setting. A how-to manual is also planned for this site.

New Weather & Disasters: Blizzards, Typhoons, Earthquakes, Tsunamis, Volcanoes

  • Concept for blizzards in cold biomes — they cut visibility and slow you down, but mainly they drive your body temperature down. The aim is a reason to head indoors rather than instant death, and shelter, light, and insulation are meant to take the edge off.
  • Concept for typhoons — directional high winds that travel over time, pushing on anything airborne, light, or falling, with heavier rain. Existing vehicles, trains, and flight are meant to keep working through them.
  • Concept for earthquakes — the screen shakes, things fall, and structures take localized damage. The damage radius is planned to be configurable down to zero, so servers with existing builds can still turn them on.
  • Concept for tsunamis — waves that push inland from the coast. They are built on the new finite-volume water, so the water that comes in actually goes back out. Linking them to earthquakes is under consideration.
  • All four are planned to be individually switchable in the config, and their frequency is to be published as measured numbers rather than "occasionally".
  • Concept for volcanoes that erupt with a low probability when an earthquake hits. The chance is planned to be configurable and published as a measured number, and there must always be a way out that avoids irreversibly wrecking an existing world.

Everyday Systems: Food Spoilage, Refrigerators, and Gasoline

  • Concept to make all food spoil if left alone long enough (the same idea as ARK). Freshness is meant to be tracked per individual item.
  • A refrigerator is planned — food kept inside spoils more slowly, or not at all. The rollout is meant to be handled so that nobody's existing food stores rot overnight the moment it lands.
  • Concept to add gasoline — every vehicle will need it. Trains are excluded.
  • Refuelling, a fuel gauge, and what happens when you run dry are all to be settled before it ships. Existing vehicles must not seize up the moment it lands, and you should never burn fuel while a typhoon is holding you still.

Pollution & the Air Purifier

  • Concept for nuclear contamination — an area hit by a nuclear warhead stays contaminated for 20 in-game days. A gas mask and a hazmat suit together grant full immunity; missing either piece means damage and debuffs.
  • Concept for air pollution — running a nuclear generator continuously fouls the air over a wide radius, and players in it take debuffs. The radius and debuff interval are planned to be configurable.
  • A new Air Purifier block is planned — it prevents and clears air pollution within its effective radius. Several can run at once to cover a larger area, and a polluted area is expected to become safe in about 60 seconds by default.
  • The purifier is planned to require power or fuel consistent with the mod's existing energy system.

Guns Mod: Balance, New Audio, and Missile Launchers

  • Under consideration: minigun +50%, assault rifle +75%, pistol ×1.5, SMG damage halved but magazine +30%, rocket launcher ×3 damage and explosion power, and turrets ×4 damage with +70% durability so mobs can't break them easily.
  • Concept to scale the nuclear warhead up to destroying as many as 10,000,000 blocks, and to triple both the explosion power and the radius of the warhead missile.
  • Planned fix for the rocket launcher blocking your own view with its model while scoped.
  • Concept to overhaul all gun audio. Existing game audio will not be reused as-is — original sounds in a similar style are to be produced instead.
  • Concept to add three new missile launcher designs, all of which a player can board, aim, and fire.

Boss Mod: Four New Bosses

  • Volcanic Golem King (600 HP) is planned — summoned by using a molten crown on the forge altar core. It turns enraged at 66% health and enters its critical phase at 33%.
  • Abyssal Kraken Warden (800 HP) is planned — woken by using a sonar eye on the sculk heart, and it goes into a frenzy at 30% health.
  • Prismatic Ice Phoenix Dragon (750 HP) is planned — it appears when a primal egg is placed on the frost altar, moving from perched to flying, and ascending at 40% health.
  • Witherstorm Demon Overlord (1,200 HP) is planned — it needs a soul blood altar, a nether star, and three wither skulls, and all three of the other new bosses must already have been slain. It enters its final phase at 25% health.
  • Each of the four is planned to get its own loot table, advancements, attack patterns, and colour palette.

Boss Mod: Existing Boss Adjustments

  • Concept to give the Plague Doctor a 20-second gap between taking damage and summoning the Witherstorm. Kill him in one hit, or inside 20 seconds, and no Witherstorm appears — but you lose the drops as well.
  • Under consideration for the Grim Reaper: a low chance to drop a Soul-Reaping Scythe on death, and a low chance for anything killed with that scythe to drop its spawn egg. The scythe is planned to be recoloured so it is easy to tell apart from the ordinary one.
  • The Grim Reaper itself is also slated for +15% strength and three new attack patterns.
  • Concept to buff the Witherstorm overall, adding destructive attacks and a second-phase form change.

Carrying Things: Villagers, Small Mobs, and Chests

  • Concept to let you pick up and carry villagers, and to let chickens and other small mobs be picked up by hand. Setting them down again won't hurt them.
  • Concept to let chests be carried with both hands without breaking them, contents intact. Large (double) chests will need two players to lift.

Money: ATM, Mine Bank, Prices, and a Diamond Balance

  • Concept to raise the interest cap to 10,000,000 emeralds.
  • Concept for a major ATM UI overhaul in the Glimpse Alpha Glass style.
  • Concept for a new Mine Bank screen: a netherite-blue dashboard with a glowing digit display, a deposit/withdraw toggle, quick-pick chips (25% / 50% / 75% / MAX) calculated from your balance, a vertical 1 / 10 / 100 / 1k stepper with a drag track, currency tabs, a fee preview, and confirm/cancel buttons.
  • Concept to raise prices everywhere by 10× to 100×. The multiplier is rolled once per product and then never moves, so prices won't shift on you right as you go to buy.
  • It applies to every price this mod pack sets. Vanilla villager trading is left alone, so the usual ways of earning emeralds stay as they are.
  • Singapore real estate (currently 1,000,000–10,000,000 emeralds) is included, which puts its effective price at 10,000,000–1,000,000,000 — above the interest cap, so interest alone will no longer get you there. The whole system is planned to be switchable off in the config.
  • Concept to add a diamond balance to the ATM. It is kept separate from the emerald balance, so an account has two lines — emeralds and diamonds. Diamonds are valued above emeralds, at some number of emeralds each.
  • Interest on the diamond balance, the per-withdrawal cap, and which account types (personal / shared / special) carry one are all to be decided from measurements and published. If exchange is offered, round-tripping emeralds to diamonds and back will not gain you a single one.

Planarcadia and the Sparxie Stick

  • Concept for an entrance to Planarcadia — build a frame out of portal amethyst blocks in the same shape as a nether portal and light it. It is meant to be something players can make themselves, from obtainable materials.
  • Because it follows nether-portal conventions, the allowed frame dimensions, how it's lit, the way back, and how coordinates map across are all to be settled explicitly and published.
  • The Sparxie Downvote Stick is planned — 200 melee damage, and the same 32 uses of durability as a golden sword. Right-click fires a projectile, at a cost of 1 durability per shot.
  • The stick is to be sold in Planarcadia at a list price of 200,000 emeralds. It is subject to the 10×–100× price rise, though, so what you actually pay is expected to land between 2,000,000 and 20,000,000 emeralds.

The Backrooms

  • Concept to completely redesign the Backrooms monsters from new design art, built with the pack's hybrid 3D-model-plus-pixel-art method and displayed in game at a 64×64 resolution.
  • The sickly-yellow palette is to be applied consistently, with an ambient particle layer over the top.

Fixes & Spec Changes: The Angel's Shield and the Masks

  • Planned fix for the Angel's Shield not working as a shield — you can raise it but it doesn't block. The cause has been identified, and it is to be fixed in line with the angel gear's own multiplier conventions.
  • Concept to change how the Sparkle and Sparxie masks work: use up around 800 durability and the mask breaks, then becomes usable again 320 seconds later. That revival only happens once — the second break is permanent.
  • A broken mask isn't destroyed, and the fact that it has already come back once stays with that specific mask — across relogs, death, chests, and server restarts. Repairing it on an anvil won't get you around the one-time limit.
