Coming next

Nothing on this page is in your game yet. These are the things being built, and the things expected in the next release, told before they are settled.

Planned No date yet

V4.2.3 — Filter Underworks and the Service Drop: straight down under the city

The update after V4.2.2. Where V4.2.2 sent you east, this one sends you down. No new dimension, no new boss. The Filter Ring plaza was built on top of a sealed sump. The drop still works. The pumps do not. Timing and contents may change.

  • Filter Underworks: a new area directly under the plaza, y 40–63, radius 96. The grace of the plaza lamps does not reach below y 64 — Residue Sickness starts after 10 seconds unmasked, 40 with a mask
  • The Service Drop: descend the shaft from the grate at the centre of the plaza into the Sump Hall (21 × 12 × 21), then walk the east tunnel at y 50 out to x=128 and climb the first viaduct pier onto the V4.2.2 deck — one continuous route
  • Service Cage: a powered hop between plaza and sump. It needs rotational power or a redstone signal. With no charge it says so, and the ladder always works
  • Condensate becomes a real fluid you can carry in a bucket. It can be placed in the Filter Ring and the Underworks; on the Flats and in the Vein Pit it boils off. Contact gives Sump Damp (6 seconds of slower movement and mining)
  • Pipe Leech: hides in the pressure mains overhead and drops when you walk under. It leaves condensate vials, and a service key 20% of the time — but the first key is a guaranteed find in the locker at x=64, so you never have to hunt one
  • Service Key (opens and closes the grate) and Sump Gauge (counts the mains near you on the action bar), plus the command /fallout drop. No new viaducts on the surface

Planned No date yet

V4.2.4 — Ring Main and Lamp Pressure: the light was never magic

The update after V4.2.3. The Filter Ring lamps have always burned condensate pushed around the wall. If the main runs dry the ring goes dark, and Filter Grace dies with it. Timing and contents may change.

  • Ring Gallery: a ledge inside the Filter Ring wall, so you can walk the wall all the way round
  • Ring Main: a loop of pressure mains and risers inside the wall. The Sump Hall pool is the header, and a header tank holds the condensate
  • Lamp pressure: filter lamps gain two states, lit and pressurised. Let the main run dry and the lamps go out — and the Grace in that stretch goes out with them
  • Header tank, pressure valve (redstone or sneak-use), a main schematic that reads the pressure, and the command /fallout pressure

Planned No date yet

V4.2.5 — Cradle Economy and Colossus Phases: the colossus is the ring's seized motor

The update after V4.2.4. The Assembly Colossus was never a random titan — it is the Filter Ring's seized motor. You walk down from the south gate, you pay residue and condensate to hold Grace at the rim, and you strip the body for parts the viaduct and the tank can use. Timing and contents may change.

  • Pit Cut: a marked stair from the Filter Ring's south gate down to the pit rim, and a dressed Cradle Floor of dark iron-concrete plate at the bottom
  • The colossus phases rewritten: what changes between phases is how it fights, not how much health it has. The sound drops a step at each phase
  • Seizure Core: one unique drop from the body. A salvage bay and a cradle chisel break it down into parts the viaduct and the header tank can use
  • Rim Beacon: a 20-second pulse of Grace, three uses. It only works at y 56 or above, inside the ring or on the cut's stairs

Planned No date yet

V4.2.6 — Ship Freeze and QA Gate: no new place to walk

The update after V4.2.5. It adds nowhere to walk. It is the round that makes V4.2 through V4.2.5 genuinely shippable. Timing and contents may change.

  • Tag pass: every block, item and entity sits in the right tool and category tags — no more blocks that nothing can mine and no decorations the wrong tool breaks
  • Command book: one tester item that lists every /fallout verb in one place
  • Language audit: the Japanese and English key sets match exactly, with no half-written strings left anywhere
  • Regression pack and install notes: the city's maths and the pressure and core rules get measured by machine every time, and the install guide states versions, the command table and anything deliberately skipped

Planned No date yet

V4.2.7 — Hotfix on Freeze: from a frozen city to an operable one

The update after V4.2.6. V4.2.6 froze the city; this one makes the frozen city operable. Timing and contents may change.

  • A config file: turn falling debris and pipe leeches on or off without rebuilding the mod
  • A worldgen budget: the viaduct, the ring main and the underworks get a cap on work per chunk so they cannot stall chunk generation
  • A tooltip sweep: every item in this mod gains a one-line note on what it is for — the same class of gap that left nine usage texts invisible in V4.0.0
  • Crash guards on three paths: a missing dimension, a block with no data behind it, and a client-only class touched on the server

Planned No date yet

V4.3.0 — Stackworks: the city only looked industrial. Now it runs

The first update of the V4.3 line, after V4.2.7. The V4.2 line built a city that looks industrial. V4.3.0 makes it actually run. West of the Filter Ring stands the factory that used to feed the header tank and the viaduct. Ten features, no more.

  • Stackworks: a new area west of the Filter Ring, and the Cinder Spur, the route that reaches it
  • A residue line in three stages: crush, wash, press. Condensate becomes a fluid the pipes officially carry
  • Two freight stations (no train is placed for you — running one is your job) and a packaging bay that feeds them
  • A seizure gearbox (tainted, high output), a residue firebox (a dirty heat source), a mask assembly line and the ashen vault

Planned No date yet

V4.3.1 — Disaster Hotfix and Industrial Twenty: an ashen winter throws grit, not snow

The update after V4.3.0. Two workstreams in one patch. One is disaster — a season clock arrives, and blizzards come only in winter. The other is the factory — the west yard grows into a real production line. Timing and contents may change.

  • A season clock: the Ashen Conurbation gains seasons. Blizzards happen in winter only, and what falls is grit, not pretty snow
  • A main-break flood: water and condensate spill out — and they always recede. No water that rises and never leaves
  • Twenty factory additions: mostly machines, recipes and facades, with exactly one small hall added. The west yard does not become a second city
  • The disaster IDs and the factory IDs are kept apart. They arrive in one update, but they are two separate lines

Planned No date yet

V4.3.2 — Industrial Twenty and Three Chassis: the factory starts building the city's cars

The update after V4.3.1. The Ashen Conurbation's land vehicles get cleared out, and only the three the factory builds remain. Gasoline, aircraft and rails stay as they are. Timing and contents may change.

  • Three chassis: the Rosso Runner (a mid-engine GT), the Toro Wedge (a wide super) and the Stern Saloon (a heavy saloon). All three drink gasoline
  • The Ashen Sparrow: one light airframe. Aircraft stay
  • A land-vehicle clear-out: every Ashen Conurbation land vehicle other than those three is removed. Rails remain the railway's business
  • The industrial twenty: what arrives is twenty items, not twenty machines

Timing and contents can change, and anything here may be dropped. Only what actually ships appears in the changelog.
