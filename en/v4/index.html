The next update

V4

20 modules

V4 is this pack's next major update. A block that stops time, a locator that draws the sound around you, a mirror that shows another dimension - 20 modules are planned. This page is an announcement made before release, and it is up for a limited time.

This page plays music. The button top-right stops it at any time.

Group 1

Machines and storage

An anchor that freezes time, a pouch that is effectively bottomless, a generator that runs on the temperature difference between biomes, a rig that sinks to bedrock, and a fabricator that pulls ingredients from the chest next door and builds on its own.

Group 2

Movement and gear

A locator that outlines where a sound came from, rail that runs six times vanilla speed, boots that turn a fall into an explosion, a rod that swaps you with whatever it hits, an armour set that steps through walls, and a turret that turns toward a noise and fires.

Group 3

Space and environment

A beam that moves items and fluid sixteen blocks, a lamp you tune by colour to grow crops, a region where gravity runs backwards, a mirror onto another dimension, a sphere that switches off redstone and magic, and a tower that harvests fluid out of the sky.
